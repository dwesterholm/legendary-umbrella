<objective>
Build the local browser rung (WATCH-01, D-01..D-05, R-02, R-03, R-10) and prove it end to end on one real Södermalm search page for free.

Identity model, stated once (assumption-delta: one transport becomes two renderers): `transport.ts` stays the single entry point (`runPlaywrightRender`) and now owns renderer selection. A script installs the local renderer with `setRenderer()`; with nothing installed, the call goes to `runApifyRender`, which is the old body moved verbatim. Apify becomes a renderer, the fallback rung, and the in-app job keeps using it unchanged because the app never installs anything.

Purpose: every Booli read in the headless scripts becomes free and fast (about 5 s instead of 120-245 s and 0.04-0.06 USD per page) without forking a single parser. Everything later in this phase (reading every candidate, BRF pages, the weekly run) depends on this.
Output: transport seam, `local-browser.ts`, a free probe CLI, parity and fake-launcher tests, `--renderer` / `BOOLI_RENDERER` / `--allow-apify` in the two headless scripts, the shared ledger settle delay (WR-05), the pinned runs-API ledger line, and `playwright` pinned in devDependencies.
