# Phase 14.2 Plan 03: 14.1 Review Fixes Summary

**Four 14.1 review warnings fixed with regression tests (reno-tier negation scope, negated dated words, "genomgående" as layout only, empty-then-error slutpris pages), plus a canonical stated-sun phrase list for the sun coverage measurement.**

## Performance

- **Duration:** about 6 min
- **Completed:** 2026-10-13
- **Tasks:** 3
- **Files modified:** 6 (3 source, 3 test)

## Accomplishments

- Reno classifier: a condition claim that praises a renovation is no longer priced as dated, and a dated kitchen is no longer hidden by a "no renovation need" phrase. For one kitchen that is the difference between 0 and 200 000 kr in the bundle.
- Sun: "genomgående renoverad" no longer earns the through-flat sun bonus (about 35 000 kr of unearned profit on a 3.8 Mkr flat, aimed at the freshly renovated flats a reno hunt wants to rank lower).
- `statedSunPhrases` lets plan 05 count how many listings state any sun at all. It returns canonical names only and infers nothing from street names. `taste.ts` is unchanged, so the taste bonus does not move.
- Slutpris puller: a page that is empty once and then errors twice is now `failed` and gets rendered again on resume, instead of silently ending a district slice in the paid cache.

## Decisions Made

- WR-03: the layout-noun-only form was chosen over the condition-word lookahead (doc comment in `sun-path.ts` says so). A bare "genomgående" followed by a word nobody listed would still get through the lookahead form.
- WR-02: the no-need phrase is removed from the text instead of returning `ok` early. The precedence order is negated renovation, dated, renovated, ok, as before.
- Intervening words allowed between a negation and "renoverat" are a fixed auxiliary/adverb list (alls, längre, nyligen, helt, nyss, vara, varit, blivit, har, är, verkar, ut, att), up to four, so "ser inte ut att vara renoverat" (four words) still works.
