# Phase 14.2 Plan 07: BRF Debt and the "Varför här" Column Summary

**Every flagged row in the weekly table now says why it might be cheap (bidding round, price cut, days listed, avgift against the district median, association debt per kvm read from Booli's public BRF page, tomträtt, layout) or says "oförklarat fynd"; on the free Södermalm run 18 of 22 flagged rows got a reason and 4 stayed unexplained, and 28 of the 30 BRF pages read had both loans and area.**

## Performance

- **Duration:** about 14 min (13:25 to 13:39 local), including 8 probe pages and one free 35-flat run
- **Tasks:** 3, **commits:** 3
- **Files:** 13 (7 created, 6 modified)
- **Spend:** none. Local renderer only (96 pages in the smoke), no Anthropic call (`--no-vision`), `Apify-reserv: 0 renderingar`.

## Accomplishments

- **BRF page reader.** `/bostadsrattsforening/<digits>` is opened through the local browser, the visible text is read, and the loans ("Lån"), the tenant-owner floor area ("Boarea bostadsrätter") and the annual-report year are parsed. Debt per kvm = loans / area, in whole kronor. Booli prints its own per-kvm figure only behind a login, so we compute it. Allabrf is not used.
- **The rules.** `watch-reasons.ts` is a pure module with one thresholds table (8 numbers, all guesses, named as such) and a fixed print order: upplåtelseform, tomträtt, nyproduktion, budgivning pågår, prissänkt, ute i N dagar, hög avgift, hög föreningsskuld, ingen hiss angiven, ovanlig planlösning, snart till salu (ej budgivning än). The last one is informational (the asking price has not been tested by bidding), counts as an explanation and always sorts last.
- **The column and the numbers around it.** "Varför här" sits after "Flaggor" in live and replay, the tracked-listing block prints it, and the coverage line gets two new items in the pinned shape. The seven plan-05 items are unchanged.

In plain terms: before this plan a row said "dold-defekt-risk, kredit-tak" and nothing more. Now Hornsgatan 119 says "tomträtt, ovanlig planlösning" and Erstagatan 21 says "hög avgift (1 481 kr/kvm/år mot 899 i Södermalm), hög föreningsskuld (25 590 kr/kvm)". The rows that stay "oförklarat fynd" are the ones worth reading by hand, because none of the checkable facts explains the price.

## How many BRF pages exposed loans and area

Two measurements, both local and free:

- **Probe** (`npx tsx scripts/probe-local-booli.ts brf <id>`, 0 USD): 8 associations from the Södermalm set, **8 of 8 had loans and area** (5 of 5 for the first five, which are the ones the plan asked for).
- **Smoke run** (below): 30 associations read, **28 of 30 had loans and area (93 %)**. BRF 81963 and BRF 80358 rendered with the area (1 186 m² and 2 600 m², report year 2023) but **without a "Lån" line**, so their debt per kvm is null. The "label missing" case therefore exists on the live site, and the parser handled it as designed (null, no crash, not counted as failed). The probe sample missed it; the committed fixtures are the three probed pages with all labels, and the missing-loan case is covered by a unit test that strips the loan lines from a real fixture.

## Issues Encountered

- **"ovanlig planlösning" is noisy.** It fires on 10 of 35 flats. Eight are one-room flats of 35 to 43,5 kvm and two are 1,5-room flats of 46 to 46,5 kvm, all above the 30 kvm-per-room limit (kvm per room across the 36 rows runs 17.5 to 43.5 with one 9.0 outlier). The thresholds are the plan's locked guesses (A5) and are untouched; the printed distribution is what the module's doc comment says to tune on. Suggest plan 09 or Daniel decides whether the upper limit should depend on the number of rooms (a one-room flat cannot be "unusual" at 38 kvm) before the weekly email is trusted.
- **"hög avgift" medians are small.** Södermalm has 35 rows, median 899 kr/kvm/år. Kungsholmen, Östermalm and Vasastan were not run in this smoke; they need 5 rows each to get a median, which a full four-district run supplies.

- **Tracked-listing block:** Folkungagatan 61 now prints "Varför här: oförklarat fynd" in the live run; in the pre-14.2 replay fixture it prints "-" because that snapshot has no facts (an old snapshot reads as "no facts", by design).
