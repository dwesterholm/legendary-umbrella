# Phase 14.6 Plan 11: Stambyte reason line and confounder entries Summary

**"stambyte pågår" / "stambyte planerat 2027" in Varför här (with "redan i värderingen" only when the model kept its stambyte term) and stambyte_ongoing / stambyte_planned in the in-app confounder guard, so a cheap flat with a coming stambyte is explained, not called a bargain**

## Accomplishments

- The weekly table's Varför här column now says "stambyte pågår", "stambyte planerat 2027" or, with no year known, "stambyte planerat" for a building whose stambyte status is "pagar" or "planerat". It sits after "hög föreningsskuld" and before "ingen hiss angiven"; "snart till salu (ej budgivning än)" is still last. A finished stambyte ("genomfort") and an unknown status give no line.
- The line ends with " (redan i värderingen)" only when the loaded model's stambyte term was kept. A model with no term, or one the cross-validation rejected, gives the bare line, so the table never claims the valuation prices a stambyte it does not.
- The in-app guard has two new known-factor ids with Swedish labels ("stambyte pågår", "stambyte planerat"). It reads an optional `stambyte` field on its input, and maps the annual report's `brf.stambytePlanerat === "planerat"` to `stambyte_planned`. "nyligen_genomfort", "ej_nämnt", null and any stray string such as "toString" add nothing.
- On a flagged row a stambyte now counts as an explanation: two flagged fixture rows through the real table builder give `{ explained: 1, unexplained: 1, flagged: 2 }`.
