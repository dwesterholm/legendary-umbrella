<domain>
Stockholm's Bygg- och plantjänsten (the city's building-permit e-service, `etjanster.stockholm.se/byggochplantjansten/arendeochhandlingar`) lists every building case per fastighet without a login, back to 1929: case group, a one-line ärendemening, start date, beteckning, address, diarienummer; the detail page adds every decision with date and outcome ("Lov med startbesked Bifall", "Slutbesked Bifall"). The search is a plain GET (`?ExtendedAddress=false&RealEstateName=<beteckning>`), fuzzy (filter exact matches), and the same page resolves a beteckning from an address. Extended search takes keywords + stadsdel + date windows. Drawings need e-legitimation (out of scope). No API, no open data, no stated terms, robots.txt 404.

Two value signals come from one source: (1) balcony permits (new balconies, glazing, balcony doors) — a granted permit without slutbesked means the building is getting balconies the asking price may not include; (2) stambyte is notifiable work (MÖD 2020:19), so the list gives a dated stambyte history per building, joinable to the 7 379 sold flats → the price effect can be MEASURED (the circulating −8–15 % / +5–12 % figures are an unsourced blog post the repo's July note repeated).

Today the repo reads stambyte only from manually uploaded annual reports (`src/lib/brf/extract.ts` enum planerat / nyligen_genomfört / ej_nämnt, no year) → `report/flags.ts` STAMBYTE_* flags, `confounder-guard.ts` prose; the discovery path's source is Allabrf, which never worked, so it is always empty. The broker text ("Stambyte genomfört 2018", "uppmätt 2019", "areauppgift enligt föreningen") is read for sun phrases and takhöjd and then dropped. Balconies are read as a Booli flag only.

Uppmätning: the measuring standard has barely changed since 1987; the extra kvm come from pre-standard figures that left out inner walls (≤ 30 cm each counts) and wardrobes. Nobody publishes how often or by how much; one stated kvm ≈ 100–115 kkr at this price level. A balcony never counts as boarea (glazed or not).
</domain>
