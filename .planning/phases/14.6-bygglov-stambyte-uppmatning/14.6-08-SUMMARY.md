# Phase 14.6 Plan 08: Broker-text stambyte and area-source readers with a precedence merge Summary

**Two pure, deterministic readers turn the broker text into a stambyte year/plan/ongoing/relining reading and an area-source label (uppmätt, förening, ekonomisk plan, lägenhetsregister, säljare, ej kontrollmätt), and mergeStambyte decides the winner per field as city > annual report > broker text, with no text kept and no model call.**

## What the readers accept and reject (plain words)

**Stambyte reader** (`stambyteFromText`)
- Accepts: "Stambyte genomfört 2018", "VA-stambyte klart 2014", "Stammarna byttes 2016", "Byte av stammar genomfördes 2016", "2017 genomfördes ett stambyte", "stambyte planeras 2027" (planned), a year above the run year ("Stambyte 2031 enligt underhållsplanen" is planned), "Stambyte pågår" (ongoing, years ignored), and ranges ("2011-2012" gives 2012).
- Rejects (reads as nothing): decades ("på 1980-talet"), years outside 1950-2045, "Inget stambyte är planerat", "Stambyte ej genomfört", "Kommande stambyte, år ej fastställt", a year that belongs to another job ("Nytt kök och badrum 2021, stambyte 2018" gives 2018, "Stambyte genomfört, nytt kök 2021" gives nothing), "istället för stambyte".
- Relining ("relinade", "relining", "rörrenovering") sets only its own flag and never a year; "stambyte genom relining" gives no year either.
- Case counts: 32 tuned phrases (all pass) plus the empty text; 38 held-back; 24 in a second set written afterwards; 6 pathological 50 000-character inputs (slowest 9 ms measured, limit 50 ms).

**Area reader** (`areaFactsFromText`)
- Accepts: "uppmätt 2019" (year kept, a measuring-firm word sets only a yes/no), "Areauppgift enligt föreningen", "enligt ekonomisk plan", "enligt lägenhetsregistret", "enligt säljaren", "Ej uppmätt" (only when nothing else is found).
- Rejects: a measurement of something else (köket, balkongen, förrådet, takhöjden, garderoben), "Mätning rekommenderas av köparen", "uppmätt av säljaren" as a measurement (it is a seller figure), a future year ("uppmätt 2087" keeps the measurement but drops the year), and "ej kontrollmätt" never replaces a stated source.
- Case counts: 36 tuned + 14 held-back from the research (50 phrases), 17 more in a second set, 6 pathological inputs (slowest 16 ms measured, including the audit masker), plus rule tests. The test file has 73 phrase rows (the plan asked for at least 30).
- `areaAuditLines`: "Boarean är uppmätt av Svensk Areamätning 2019." prints as "Boarean är uppmätt av ▒ ▒ 2019."; names, e-mail addresses and long digit runs are masked; each line is cut at 160 characters. It is for terminal printing only.
- `areaUpsideSek`: 3 695 000 kr and 35 kvm give 105 571 kr (one kvm) and 211 143 kr (two kvm), only for a non-measured source in a building from before 1987, otherwise nothing. Nothing in this plan feeds the fit score, taste, flags or the model.

## The precedence rule (mergeStambyte)

Per field, the city's case list (`bygglov_stockholm`) beats the annual report (`arsredovisning`) beats the broker text (`maklartext`), and a source wins a field only with a positive finding. A city list that found no stambyte never erases a year the broker or the report states. Status order is pågår, then planerat, then genomfört, and the source shown is the source of the field the status came from. The annual report has no year: "planerat" gives status planerat and no planned year, "nyligen_genomfort" gives genomfört and no year, "ej_nämnt" gives nothing. The annual-report value goes through a `Map`, so "toString" and any other non-enum string give nothing.
