<objective>
Collect the city's dated stambyte history for the whole inner city in one polite sweep (STAM-01, D-05, R-01).

Purpose: the price model can only measure what a stambyte does to the price if it knows which sold flats sat in a building with a recorded stambyte, and when. One search returns every matching case for a stadsdel and a time window, so about a hundred requests cover all of innerstan since 1985. This plan fetches and caches that history and writes the counts; plan 06 joins it to the sold flats.
Output: src/lib/discovery/stambyte-join.ts (+test), scripts/bygglov-sweep.ts, evidence/sweep.json and evidence/sweep.md.
</objective>
