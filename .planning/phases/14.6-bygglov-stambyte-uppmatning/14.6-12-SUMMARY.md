Five more addresses have their property found but not their list. The same command finishes them, about 25 requests.
- **Folkungagatan 61** is Droskhästen 8. The Stambyte column reads **2006** (the year the stambyte case was opened; the final sign-off was not read, so the work may have finished a little later). Byggärenden reads "vind 2023-10, hiss 2021-12": an attic conversion (signed off 2025-03) and a lift.
- **Stambyte (replacing the water and drain pipes)**: 15 addresses show something. 13 have a year, between 1990 and 2016, and none is newer than 2016. ### What the model measured against the folklore

The folklore is "a stambyte before costs 8 to 15 percent, after adds 5 to 12 percent". It comes from one blog post with no source. I measured it on 7 321 sold flats whose building I could look up, of which 2 399 had a stambyte on record before the sale.

| Years from the building's latest recorded stambyte to the sale | Flats | Price per kvm against buildings with none on record |
|---|---|---|
| 0 to 10 | 83 | +2,4 % (uncertainty ±1,3) |
| 11 to 15 | 145 | -1,8 % (±1,0) |
| 16 to 25 | 850 | +1,2 % (±0,4) |
| 26 to 40 | 1 316 | +1,0 % (±0,3) |

Nothing here comes near ten percent, and the signs point in different directions. "Before" cannot be measured at all: "no stambyte on record" mixes buildings that never did one, buildings about to, and buildings that did one the city never heard about. "After" within five years has 5 flats in the whole data set, so +5 % can neither be confirmed nor ruled out; within ten years the data says about +2 % give or take 1.

**Does the model use it? No.** The planned test (five-fold cross-validation, where the model is fitted on four fifths of the sales and scored on the fifth it did not see) passed, but by 0,07 percent. When I compare the two models flat by flat, the gain is 0,9 standard errors, and about 2 is needed before it can be told apart from luck. You set the rule that a result is measured, not tuned, so the term stays out (decision R-08). `data/hedonic-model.json` has the same checksum before and after this plan and is identical to the file from before the phase.

What it would have done to today's candidates: 60 of 77 priced flats move, between -0,3 % and +2,1 %; 32 flats change rank; 6 of the top 15 places change. Folkungagatan 61 would go from place 16 to 14 (resale +46 537 kr, profit +43 638 kr). The comparison is in `evidence/valuation-compare-stambyte/`. If you ever want it in, the exact command is in `evidence/model-swap.md`; it moves only the stambyte term and refuses if anything else differs. Run it after 14.5's plans 12 and 13, because their replays compare against the current file.

One more thing the data shows: buildings with a recorded stambyte also tend to have less BRF debt (correlation -0,15) and lower monthly fees (-0,07). The model already prices those two at about +0,7 % together, so a stambyte effect and a healthy-finances effect are hard to pull apart.

The Stambyte and Byggärenden columns, the "stambyte pågår/planerat" reason line and the in-app explanation all ship regardless, as decided.

### Area source: what is known about coverage

There is no real-text coverage yet. The area reader (which tells you whether the broker's square metres are measured, from the association, from the economic plan and so on) and the stambyte text reader have only been scored on invented phrases:

- Stambyte reader: on a set I wrote after the reader was finished, it captured 16 of 24 on first contact (67 %), 18 of 24 after two general fixes. Expect about two statements in three.
- Area reader: 9 of 17 on first contact (53 %), 10 of 17 after one fix. Expect about half. A miss gives "okänd" or a weaker source, never a false "uppmätt".

The check against 40 real broker texts waits for the next live run that reads broker text, as you planned (texts are never stored). Add `--area-audit` to that run, read the masked sentences in the terminal and write only the counts into `evidence/area-audit.md`. Until then the Area column says "okänd" for every flat.

One wording gap from plan 10 is fixed: the note said "ofta 1–3 kvm" while the kr upside covers one and two square metres. Both now say 1–2 kvm ("Kan vara större vid uppmätning (ofta 1–2 kvm, ej säkert)"). The kr figure is unchanged and still outside the score.
