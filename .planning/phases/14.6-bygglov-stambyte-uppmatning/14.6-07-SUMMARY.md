# Phase 14.6 Plan 07: The Stambyte Price Effect Summary

**A recorded stambyte moves the price by between -1,8 and +2,4 % against buildings with none on record; the plan's cross-validation rule keeps the term, but by 0,07 % (t = 0,9), so it sits in a scratch model and the committed model is untouched.**

## Performance

- **Duration:** about 15 min
- **Completed:** 2026-10-18
- **Tasks:** 3
- **Files modified:** 8 (3 code, 5 evidence)

## What was measured

7 321 sold flats whose building was identified (after outlier removal); 2 399 (32,8 %) had a stambyte recorded before the sale, 22 had one under way (too few to fit, bar is 30). The data picked the age groups: only 5 rows sit within 5 years of the sale, so the groups became 0-10, 11-15, 16-25 and 26-40 years. D-06's nominal 0-5, 5-15 and 15-40 would have had 5, 223 and 2 166 rows.

| Years from latest recorded stambyte to sale | Rows | Against no stambyte on record |
|---|---|---|
| 0-10 | 83 | +2,4 % (SE 1,3) |
| 11-15 | 145 | -1,8 % (SE 1,0) |
| 16-25 | 850 | +1,2 % (SE 0,4) |
| 26-40 | 1 316 | +1,0 % (SE 0,3) |
| Over 40, or none recorded (reference) | 4 927 | - (stored as -0,3 % against the mean of all identified rows, SE 0,2) |

Stored in the model, each group is measured against the mean of all identified rows (+2,1 %, -2,2 %, +0,9 %, +0,7 %), so a flat in an unidentified building gets 0.

- **Cross-validation (5 folds):** without 0,1078, with 0,1077 (0,1078 -> 0,1077; exact 0,107796 -> 0,107724). The error drops 0,07 %; the row-by-row paired t is 0,9. By the plan's strict rule the term is kept. Statistically it is not distinguishable from nothing.
- **Correlation of "recorded stambyte" (1/0):** with debt per kvm r = -0,15 (n = 2 292), with avgift per kvm and month r = -0,07 (n = 3 826). Recorded buildings carry about 1 500 kr/kvm less debt (4 729 against 6 234) and 2,8 kr/kvm/mån less avgift, which stage 1 already prices at about +0,4 % and +0,3 %. The term is fitted on what stage 1 leaves, so the same thing is not counted twice, but the two are hard to separate.
- **Folklore against the data:** "-8 till -15 % före, +5 till +12 % efter" (one unsourced blog post, Boio). "Efter": +2,4 % within ten years (SE 1,3, 83 rows, only 5 within five years) neither confirms nor rules out +5 %; 16-40 years later it is about +1 %. "Före" cannot be measured: "none recorded" mixes never-done, about-to-do and unreported.

## Decisions Made

- The verdict is the plan's: kept. The size of the gain is stated next to it everywhere (log, evidence, here) and the recommendation for plan 12 is not to graft without Daniel's yes: a term worth +-2 % that cannot be told from chance would also put "stambyte" in the imputed list of every candidate whose building is unknown.
- Stored effects are centred on the mean of all identified rows, as the plan's formula says. The report also shows each group against "none recorded", because that is the figure comparable to the folklore.
- Tied for the most buckets, the layout whose smallest bucket is biggest wins. A recorded stambyte in a building with an ongoing one counts as ongoing when that group is fitted.

**2. [Clarification] The planted-effect test checks the effect against "no stambyte on record"**
- The plan says the 16-40 year bucket's pct lies in -0,07 to -0,05. The stored pct is centred on the mean of all identified rows (the plan's own formula), which with 18 % of rows planted gives about -5 %, so the test checks the ratio to the reference group (-7 to -5 %), which is what was planted.

**3. [Stricter than plan] `--graft-stambyte` requires an explicit `--out`**, so the committed model cannot be overwritten by leaving the flag out.

**4. Task 2's pure-function tests (block order, thin data, graft) were written with Task 1**, because they exercise the same code; Task 2 added only the `cvGain` assertions. The script's refusals are covered by the plan's automated check and a graft smoke test, not by unit tests.

**5. Commit trailer** is `Claude Sonnet 5.5`, the model that ran this plan, not the "Fable 5.1" the dispatch preferred.
