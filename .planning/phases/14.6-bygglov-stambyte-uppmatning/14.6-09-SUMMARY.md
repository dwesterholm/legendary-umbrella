# Phase 14.6 Plan 09: Candidate facts and the city stage Summary

**Sixteen new facts per candidate (stambyte, building cases, area source, beteckning) filled from the broker text and from a city stage that runs live or from the cache, with a candidates runner that fills the cache using city traffic only**

## Accomplishments

- Every candidate's facts record now carries the stambyte reading (last year, planned year, ongoing, source, status, relining), the per-source readings next to the merged values, the two balcony flags, a list of up to six recent building cases, the area source, the year it was measured and a yes/no for a named measuring firm, and the building's fastighetsbeteckning with the rung that found it. Every field is nullable with a null default, so the 2026-10-13 snapshot parses unchanged and its replay ranking is identical to before (checked with the timing line filtered).
- The enrichment reads the text readers from plan 08 beside the sun phrases and the ceiling height, before `text = null`. Only derived values are stored; a test proves a name, phone number, e-mail address and the sentences themselves appear nowhere in the facts or the candidates.
- `runCityStage` adds the city's facts per candidate. Cache-only: no network at all, any valid cached entry used however old. Live: address rungs and case lists cache-first, one list per building however many candidates share it, detail pages only for interesting cases started within five years (newest first, at most three per building), saved back with the list's own fetch date. A request budget of 300, a 20-minute deadline, one row at a time, and a CityStop ends the stage with every unreached row left as it was.
- `scripts/bygglov-candidates.ts` runs the stage over a stored snapshot's rows (the snapshot is read only), fills the city cache, and prints the rung line and the coverage line. Its dry run reads saved pages with a virtual clock and a throw-away gates folder.

## Deviations from Plan

### Auto-fixed Issues

**1. [Rule 2 - Missing critical] The case number (`diarie`) in `byggarenden` is nullable**
- **Found during:** Task 1 (facts schema)
- **Issue:** The plan gave `diarie` a strict regex and no null. A case number the city writes in an unexpected form would make the whole facts parse throw, and the enrichment would then lose that candidate's facts altogether (the parse sits inside the per-candidate try).
- **Fix:** `diarie` is `regex | null`. `cityFactsFromCases` cleans the number to the allowed characters (at most 20) and gives null when nothing is left.
- **Files modified:** `src/lib/discovery/listing-facts.ts`, `src/lib/stockholm/city-stage.ts`
- **Verification:** `city-stage.test.ts` ("words a read case from its decisions and keeps only digits in the case number") and the schema test for bad months and case numbers
- **Committed in:** `dd96df6`

### Plan wording not followed literally

- The live-mode test uses "BRF Droskhästen 8" for the BRF-name rung, not "BRF Oljan 18": no saved list page exists for Oljan 18, and a confirming list needs the candidate's own address in it. The behaviour proved is the same (one list fetch, reused).
- The plan's example of a three-row run (Folkungagatan 61, an Oljan 18 row, a second Droskhästen 8 row) is covered as three rows of Droskhästen 8 plus a separate BRF-name run, a no-case building (Hornsgatan 42 gives "Ormen Mindre 2" with an empty list) and scripted clients for the caps.
- The wave-end fast-forward of `main` and `git push origin main` (plan verification) was not done, as in the earlier plans of this phase: the branch stays as checked out and a push starts CI and the E2E run on Daniel's Mac.
