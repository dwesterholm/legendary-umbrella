<objective>
Link the city's stambyte history to the 7 400 sold flats and say how many could be linked (STAM-01, D-05).

Purpose: a stambyte effect can only be measured on sold flats whose building we have identified. The free address match covers about one row in five on Södermalm; the city's address search widens it to the near misses and, if a sample says it is worth it, to everything else. The join rate is the honest headline: it tells Daniel how much of the market the measurement stands on.
Output: scripts/stambyte-join.ts, the gitignored sold-join.json, and evidence/join.json and evidence/join.md.
</objective>
