## What the join found

"Kopplad" means we know which property a sold flat is in. The city's address search places almost any address, so 99,2 % is high. It does not mean 99 % have a known stambyte. The figure that decides what the fit can do is the share in a building with a recorded stambyte before the sale.

| Stadsdel (Booli's label) | Sold rows | Tied to a building | In a building with a stambyte before the sale |
|---|---|---|---|
| Södermalm | 2 371 | 2 352 (99,2 %) | 776 (32,7 %) |
| Kungsholmen | 2 218 | 2 196 (99,0 %) | 823 (37,1 %) |
| Vasastan | 1 972 | 1 961 (99,4 %) | 590 (29,9 %) |
| Östermalm | 870 | 862 (99,1 %) | 228 (26,2 %) |
| All | 7 431 | 7 371 (99,2 %) | 2 417 (32,5 %) |

How the rows were tied:

- By address, free (38,0 % of rows): 2 122 rows on an exact address (A), 704 on the same street and number with another entrance letter (A+). Per district, stages A and A+ together: Södermalm 906 of 2 371 (38,2 %), Kungsholmen 883 of 2 218 (39,8 %), Vasastan 708 of 1 972 (35,9 %), Östermalm 329 of 870 (37,8 %). The 38,0 % counts any swept case at the address; the narrower figure that RESEARCH 5.3 estimated (a building with a stambyte case, Södermalm) is 26,1 % (deviation 8).
- By the city's address search (61,2 % of rows): 2 166 rows for the near class and for addresses where the cases named several buildings (B), 2 379 rows for the rest (C).
- Not tied: 60 rows. 5 have no house number, 39 got several buildings from the city's search (left alone on purpose), 16 got no answer.
- Of the 3 287 distinct addresses: 832 exact, 356 letter variant, 990 near, 1 104 with no case near, 5 with no number.

The sample of 100 addresses from the 1 104 with no case near found 6 whose building has a stambyte case (the bar was 5), so the full pass ran. It added 176 rows with a recorded stambyte before the sale for about 1 000 requests; stage B added 264 for about 1 019. The 16 detail pages then moved one row out of that count.

Years from the latest recorded stambyte to the sale, in rows:

| 0-5 | 6-10 | 11-15 | 16-25 | 26-40 | över 40 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 5 | 78 | 148 | 858 | 1 323 | 5 |

Also 22 rows where a stambyte was under way at the sale, 27 rows where a stambyte case started only after the sale (never counted as done), and 0 rows in a building with only relining (the register holds 2 relining cases, both in Norrmalm).

## What it means for the fit

- A fit needs about 30 rows per class. Classes 6-10 (78), 11-15 (148), 16-25 (858) and 26-40 (1 323) have enough. 6 to 15 years together is 226 rows.
- 0-5 years has 5 rows and "under way at the sale" has 22. Neither can be fitted. If a stambyte lifts the price in the first years after (the blog claim of +5 to +12 %), this data cannot show it.
- So the usable question is "stambyte 6 to 40 years ago against none recorded", not "fresh stambyte against old". That is RESEARCH 6.2's fallback.
- The "none recorded" group (about 5 000 rows) mixes buildings that never changed pipes with buildings that did and never reported it. The record is dense for 1994 to 2006 and thin after 2011 (plan 05), so "recorded" leans toward buildings that renovated in the 1990s and 2000s. Plan 07 has to read an effect with that in mind.

## Deviations from Plan

**1. [Rule 3 - Blocking] A pure module beside the script**
- **Found during:** Task 1
- **Issue:** The plan lists only the script. The stage logic, the counts and the strict evidence schema need unit tests, and the plan's own verification line runs vitest.
- **Fix:** `src/lib/discovery/sold-join.ts` and its test hold the logic; the script does the files and the traffic. The plan's key link names `classifySoldAddress` in the script; the script reaches it through `planAddresses`.
- **Committed in:** 63abc84

**2. [Rule 1 - Bug risk] `matchAddressFragment` instead of `pickFromAddressFragment`**
- **Found during:** Task 1
- **Issue:** `pickFromAddressFragment` returns only the designation, so an ambiguous answer and an empty one look the same, and both would be cached as "no_candidates". The ladder in beteckning.ts caches them differently.
- **Fix:** The script uses `matchAddressFragment`, the function `pickFromAddressFragment` wraps, and caches `ambiguous` and `no_candidates` apart. The plan's key-link pattern `pickFromAddressFragment` is therefore not in the script.
- **Committed in:** 63abc84

**3. [Rule 2 - Missing critical] Two more gitignored files**
- **Found during:** Task 2
- **Issue:** A stopped run needs the drawn sample and the per-stage counts; the detail dates need a place.
- **Fix:** `join-state.json` (requests and seconds per stage, the sample, the last stop) and `join-details.json` (start- and slutbesked dates for the 16 cases), both under the guarded cache directory.
- **Committed in:** 63abc84, d5c8af0

**4. [Rule 1 - Bug risk] The cause of a failed request was invisible**
- **Found during:** Task 2 (the third stop)
- **Issue:** The city client keeps only the error's name, so a reset and a timeout looked alike.
- **Fix:** The script's fetch wrapper logs the cause code (never a URL). The log showed ECONNRESET.
- **Committed in:** d5c8af0

**5. [Process] A restart driver outside the repo**
- A small shell script in the session's scratch folder reran a stage after a timeout or reset (90 s pause) and stopped on anything else, or after two runs with fewer than 3 answers. It is not part of the repo.

**6. [Process] 37 rows labelled B and C before the live stages ran**
- The stage-A-only run already showed 20 rows in B and 17 in C. They come from about a dozen address-map entries that plan 03's live tests left (the same addresses turn up among the sold rows). The label follows the address class, so those rows are counted in B or C without a request in this plan.

**7. [Process] Wave end not done**

**8. The Södermalm free share differs from RESEARCH by more than 3 points**
