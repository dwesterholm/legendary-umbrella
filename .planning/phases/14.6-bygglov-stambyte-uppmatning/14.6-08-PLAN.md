<objective>
Read stambyte and area-source statements out of the broker text the weekly run already holds in memory, and merge the stambyte sources by precedence (STAM-03, FLAG-02, D-07, D-09).

Purpose: brokers often write "Stambyte genomfört 2018" or "Areauppgift enligt föreningen". Today that text is read for sun phrases and the ceiling height and then thrown away. Two small, deterministic readers turn it into a year and a source label, with no text kept and no AI call. The merge decides which source wins when the city, the annual report and the broker disagree.
Output: stambyte-text.ts, area-source.ts, stambyte-facts.ts and their tests.
</objective>
