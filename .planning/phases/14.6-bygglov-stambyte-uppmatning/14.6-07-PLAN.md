<objective>
Measure what a recorded stambyte does to the sale price, and keep that number in the model only if it predicts better (STAM-02, D-06, R-03, R-04).

Purpose: the circulating "-8 to -15 % before, +5 to +12 % after" figures trace to one unsourced blog post. With the city history joined to 7 400 sales, the model can measure it. The research already warns that recorded stambyten after 2011 are thin, so the likely verdict is "not kept"; this plan makes that a clean, reported result instead of a failure, and leaves the committed model alone until plan 12.
Output: the optional stambyteTerm in hedonic-model.ts, --stambyte-join and --graft-stambyte in fit-hedonic.ts, two scratch fits, and evidence with the verdict.
</objective>
