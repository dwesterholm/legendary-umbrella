### Display-text consistency after the fixes

The two fixes that change what is shown were traced from the data to every place the text is printed.

- **"2006 (ansökt)" (WR-04).** `bygglov-classify.ts` assigns basis `ärendestart` to a stambyte year taken from a case whose detail page was never opened, `start` to an opened case, and no year to a refused or withdrawn one. The basis travels `city-stage.ts` -> `CandidateFacts.cityStambyte.basis` -> `fit-score.ts` (passed on only when the year shown is the city's own year, so a broker-text year can never be mislabelled) -> `formatStambyteCell`, which prints `<year> (ansökt)` for `ärendestart` and a bare year otherwise. The weekly mail column and the tracked-flat block use that same cell string, so they cannot disagree with the table. The real replay shows "2006 (ansökt)" for Folkungagatan 61 and 13 more table rows (1999, 2016, 2001, 1990, 2000, 2003, 1994, 1991, 2004 and others) with no bare year for an unopened case. `fit-pipeline.test.ts` pins the cell wording.
- **"(redan i värderingen)" (WR-06).** The suffix is added to "stambyte pågår" only when `coveredByModel.stambytePagar` is true (the loaded model has a kept term with an under-way group) AND the row's `stambyteSource` is `bygglov_stockholm`. "Planerat" and a broker-text "pågår" never get it. `coveredByModel` is derived in `watch-reasons.ts` from `model.stambyteTerm?.kept === true && ongoing != null`. With the committed model (no term) no stambyte line carries it, as seen in the replay. The other two uses of the phrase (avgift and föreningsskuld, which the model really fits) are unchanged: 20 occurrences in the replay, all on those two reasons.
