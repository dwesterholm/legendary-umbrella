# Phase 14.4 Plan 04: Days on market in the expected final price Summary

**A listing's days on market now lower the expected overbid (-0,035 per ln-day, kept because 5-fold error fell from 0,0736 to 0,0727 on the same held-out sales), shown in the tracked-listing block; the flat's resale value does not move with days.**

## What this means for Daniel

The data supports your idea that a flat that has sat on the market draws a smaller overbid. The model learned it from 837 sold flats that had both a days-on-market figure and an asking price, and kept it because it predicted the sales it had not seen slightly better, and the sign is the expected one (longer listing, smaller premium).

How big it is, for a typical flat asked at the model's price:

- A flat sold after 7 days is expected to go for about 10,5 % over asking.
- A flat sold after 60 days is expected to go for about 2,9 % over asking.
- That is 7,6 percentage points between a week and two months.

The days only touch the price you are expected to pay. They are not in the resale value, so a long-listed flat does not look worth less when you sell it; it looks cheaper to buy.

Folkungagatan 61 has been listed for 4 days, which is a fresh listing, so the term pushes its expected overbid up, not down:

| | Before (no days term) | After |
|---|---|---|
| Expected overbid | 17,7 % (district + price slope) | 20,2 % (district + price slope + days) |
| Expected final price | 4 348 979 kr | 4 441 627 kr |
| Profit | 1 162 183 kr | 1 123 047 kr |
| Rank in the replay | 8 of 77 | 9 of 77 |

Resale value stays 5 503 113 kr. Its expected final price is above 4 M kr, so the flag for that remains.

Three things to know before trusting the number:

- **The improvement is small.** Error in log terms went from 0,0736 to 0,0727, about 1,2 % lower. The term is statistically supported but it does not make the premium much more predictable.
- **Days and asking price share information.** A flat asked low sells fast, and a flat asked high sits. When the days term went in, the price slope in the premium roughly halved (b from -0,18 to -0,10), so some of what used to be credited to "asked below the model price" is now credited to "short listing". Together they still describe one thing, and 20,2 % for Folkungagatan 61 is the sum of both.
- **Candidates' days are "so far", sold flats' days are final.** A candidate listed for 4 days may sell on day 15, so a fresh listing is compared with sold flats that include long ones. This gives long-listed candidates a somewhat higher premium than they will end up with. It is documented in the model note and not corrected (RESEARCH Pitfall 15). The error errs toward a higher expected price, not a lower one.

The per-district premiums in the model moved up a little (Södermalm 11,0 % to 12,2 %, Kungsholmen 5,7 % to 6,4 %, Vasastan 6,1 % to 6,9 %, Östermalm 4,1 % to 5,1 %). They now mean "asked at the model price and listed for the average 14,6 days", where before they meant "asked at the model price" with no regard to days.

## Accomplishments

- `fitBidPremium` tries the days term after choosing the base, refits both on each fold's training rows, scores both on the same held-out rows, and keeps the term only when its error is strictly lower and its coefficient is negative. A rejected term is stored with `kept: false` and the note "prövad, förkastad: korsvalidering" or "prövad, förkastad: fel tecken"; the base is then exactly what it would have been. When kept, slope and intercepts are the jointly re-estimated ones.
- `expectedOverbidDetail` returns the premium with `base`, `domDays`, `domUsed`, the condition slot (0 and source "prior" while empty) and a label such as "distrikt+pris+dagar" or "distrikt+pris, dagar okända". `expectedOverbid` is now its `.pct` with days unknown, so the old values for flat and slope models are unchanged.
- `fitScore` passes the candidate's `facts.daysOnMarket` and stores `overbidDetail`; the not-comparable branch stores null.
- `scripts/fit-hedonic.ts` feeds `daysActive` into the premium rows and prints "Budpremie: dagar på marknaden" (row counts, both errors, coefficient, decision, the 7 versus 60 day effect, and what the term would have done if it had been rejected). The model note records the "so far" caveat.
- `scripts/fit-score.ts` prints `Förv. överbud: 20.2 % (distrikt+pris+dagar)` and `Dagar ute: 4 (dagar-term: med: korsvalidering 0,0736 -> 0,0727)`.
