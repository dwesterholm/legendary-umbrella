# Phase 14.4 Plan 09: Condition in the bid premium, sold-photo condition read, fit branch chosen Summary

**A candidate's picture verdict now feeds the expected overbid (dated -1, all fresh +1, else 0, with "skick ej mätt" until a fitted term exists), and a 0,44 kr Haiku smoke showed that sold-flat photos can be read, so the term will be fitted rather than left at the 0 prior.**

## Plain-words result (for Daniel)

**Can sold-flat photos be read?** Yes. Anthropic fetched the photos of 5 sold flats from Booli's image host and answered on all 5. The answer format (one of dated / ok / fresh plus a confidence) was accepted on the first try. The smoke cost 0,44 kr against a 1 kr limit. Of 1 200 sold flats read in plan 02, 463 (38,6 %) show photo ids, and 324 of those also have an asking price, which is what the bid-premium fit needs.

**Which branch.** The rule was written before the measurement: fit the condition term only if at least 200 sold flats have photos and an asking price, and at least 4 of 5 smoke reads work. The measured numbers are 324 and 5 of 5, so the branch is **fit**. The decision and its numbers are in `evidence/bid-branch.md`. Plan 10 reads up to 300 flats (at most about 30 kr in total, tracked in a ledger that survives reruns), fits the term, and keeps it only if cross-validation says the prediction error drops. If it does not, the term stays at 0 and the label stays "skick ej mätt". There is still no invented number in code: the prior for condition is 0.

**One warning for plan 10.** 2 of the 5 smoke reads came back at 0,40 confidence. The fit throws out anything under 0,6 instead of calling it "ordinary condition", because coding it 0 would claim we know the flat was ordinary. If 40 % of the 300 reads are that unsure, the fit has about 180 usable rows, below the 200 the power estimate wanted. That is a small sample (5 flats), so it is a flag to watch, not a result.

**What changes for the candidates today.** Nothing in the numbers yet, on purpose: the model has no condition term, so every row's premium is unchanged and its Budpremie cell now ends in "skick ej mätt" (Folkungagatan 61 reads "+20,2 % (distrikt+pris+dagar, skick ej mätt)"). The run header has a new second line, "Budpremie skick: ej mätt (prior 0): ingen skickterm i modellen". On the two snapshots with picture reads:

| Snapshot | Comparable rows | Dated (-1) | Fresh (+1) | Neutral (0) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| forsale-snapshot-2026-10-13 (replay baseline) | 77 | 5 | 0 | 72 |
| forsale-snapshot-14.4-layout (the live run from plan 08) | 75 | 7 | 0 | 68 |

No candidate was all-fresh on the pictures, so a fitted term would only ever lower a price here, on 5 to 7 flats. As a what-if (not stored anywhere): a fitted term of 0,02 would lower the expected final price of those flats by about 2 % each and leave the other 68 to 72 untouched.
