<objective>
Let a flat's photographed condition shape how much bidders are expected to pay over asking, and find out with real sold-flat photos whether that effect can be measured at all.

Purpose: BID-02 and the first half of BID-01 (D-12, D-13, R-08). The expected final price is asking x a premium; bidders see the same dated kitchen the model sees. On the candidate side the condition already exists (the deep picture read), so BID-02 needs no new call. On the training side the question is whether sold pages still carry photos and whether Anthropic can fetch them; plan 02 recorded photo ids for free, and a 1 kr smoke here settles the rest. Plan 10 fits the term (or records the 0 prior) and closes the phase.
Part 3 starts only after Part 2 (plans 07-08) is shipped (D-14). Spend (D-17): ≤ 1 kr for the smoke; the sold-condition budget is ≤ 30 kr in total; 0 USD Apify.
Output: condition index at prediction with the 'skick ej mätt' label, the sold-condition module with tests, the gated smoke, and the committed branch decision.
</objective>
