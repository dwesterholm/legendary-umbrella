# Phase 14.4 Plan 06: Before/after valuation report Summary

**An offline report that follows each of the 17 flats the old 20 % ceiling limited from its old credit to its new one, in kronor per cause (ceiling, refit, days, avgift, BRF debt, tomträtt, rest), with the top 15 side by side and Folkungagatan 61 broken down line by line.**

## What this means for Daniel

The 20 % ceiling used to cap how much of a flat's "cheap against the model" gap the profit could count. It is gone. This report shows what that did, on the 2026-10-13 snapshot (78 listings, 77 comparable), by replaying stored data. Nothing was fetched and no setting was changed.

**How much the ceiling used to hide.** 6,55 M kr of credit on 17 flats, of which 4,58 M kr sat inside the old top 15. Those 17 flats had a combined old credit of 19,1 M kr and have 25,8 M kr now.

**What moved those 17 flats, in kronor (sum over all 17):**

| Cause | Kr | In plain words |
|---|---:|---|
| Ceiling removed | +6 549 977 | The full gap is counted now |
| Refit (balcony, build year, other coefficients) | +1 303 594 | The model was refitted with a much larger balcony effect (+1,9 % to +8,0 %) and new build-year effects |
| Days on market in the bid premium | +257 251 | A flat that has been listed long expects a lower overbid, a short listing a higher one |
| Avgift | -1 141 438 | A high monthly fee lowers what the flat is worth |
| BRF debt | -560 003 | A high association debt lowers it |
| Tomträtt | 0 | The model could not estimate it (no leasehold sales in the data) |
| Rest | +249 437 | Other coefficients shifting when avgift and debt entered the model |

Ceiling removal dominates in total. Avgift and debt take back 1,7 M kr, about a quarter of what the ceiling hid. Per flat the picture is mixed: Erstagatan 21 loses 432 000 kr to avgift and debt together, while Idungatan 5 loses almost nothing to them and falls from rank 6 to 19 mainly because the refit and the days term took 172 000 kr off its credit.

**How the top 15 moved.** 11 of 15 are the same listings. Left the top 15: Idungatan 5 (6 to 19), Kungsklippan 20 (9 to 24), Hagaesplanaden 63 (10 to 20), Hagaesplanaden 73 (14 to 27). Came in: Fyrverkarbacken 25 (16 to 8), Pokalvägen 4 (19 to 10), Fyrverkarbacken 23 (18 to 12), Reimersholmsgatan 39 (20 to 15). The 15 profits add up to 16,96 M kr before and 24,31 M kr after. The top row, Fatburs Kvarngata 18, stays first with 2 318 010 kr.

**Folkungagatan 61 goes from rank 29 to rank 18, profit 778 545 kr to 1 123 047 kr.** It was never limited by the ceiling, so the ceiling explains none of it. What moved its credit (+329 474 kr): the refit +522 906 kr (it has a balcony and was built in 1912, and the balcony effect grew from +1,9 % to +8,0 %), the days term -164 043 kr (it has been listed only 4 days, which pushes the expected overbid up; the overbid as a whole goes from 15,7 % to 20,2 %), avgift -8 698 kr (its fee is close to the average), BRF debt 0 (no debt figure is known for its association), rest -20 691 kr. So the move up is mostly the larger balcony effect, and the new days term takes back about a third of it.

**The warning flag "ovanligt långt under modellpris".** On the default basis (expected final price against the model) it marks 10 of 77 flats; on the asking-price basis it marks 26. Of the 17 formerly capped flats, 10 carry it on the default basis and 15 on the asking basis. Switching basis is one constant (`RESIDUAL_FLAG_BASIS` in `fit-score.ts`) and changes only which rows carry the flag, never a profit figure.

**Measured versus assumed.**
- Measured: every number in the report comes from the stored records, the committed model and the snapshot. The gap each record states was recomputed from the snapshot and the model and matched within 1 kr for all rows, for the after record and for the intermediate record.
- Assumed: "kr explained by a fact" is defined as the change in the gap when that one fact is set to unknown. Because effects multiply, the parts do not have to add up on their own, so the rest is printed as "Övrigt" (+249 437 kr in total, up to about 111 000 kr on a single flat).
- Not shown: how much of "Övrigt" and "Ny anpassning" comes from the build-year effect separately from the balcony effect. That would need a second intermediate model.

**Open point for Daniel.** Two stage-2 coefficients sit exactly on the +-8 % limit the fit allows: balcony +8,0 % and build year 1961-1990 -8,0 %. The fit clamps them there, so the unclamped estimate may be larger. Nothing was changed. Because balcony drives most of Folkungagatan 61's rise, this limit matters for where it lands.

**1. [Rule 2 - Missing critical] Days term separated from the refit via the intermediate model**
- **Found during:** Task 2
- **Issue:** The intermediate model already carries plan 04's days term, so the plan's "Ny anpassning" (intermediate gap minus old gap) mixed the balcony/build-year switch with the bid-premium change. The execution notes ask for the days term as its own cause.

- **Files modified:** src/lib/discovery/valuation-report.ts, its test, scripts/valuation-report.ts
- **Verification:** the plan's task 1 and task 2 verify commands pass unchanged; the four-part sum check holds on all 17 rows
- **Committed in:** 76420cf

**2. [Rule 1 - Bug in the plan's wording] "Positive explained kr" and "parts sum to the change" cannot both hold for one field**
- **Found during:** Task 1 (writing the behavior tests)
- **Issue:** The plan says the explained kr of avgift is positive and also that avgift + debt + tomträtt + övrigt equals new gap minus intermediate gap. A feature that lowers the value is positive in the first and negative in the second.
- **Fix:** `explainedKr` returns the positive "explained" form (tested); the report fields `avgiftKr`, `debtKr`, `tomtrattKr` are the signed change in the credit so the plan's sum check passes. Both are documented in the code.
