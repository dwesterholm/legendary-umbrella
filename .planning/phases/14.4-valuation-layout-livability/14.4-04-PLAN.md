<objective>
Let how long a flat has been for sale move its expected final price (not its value), and keep that term only if the data says it helps.

Purpose: VAL-03 (D-02 as amended by R-01). A long listing is a consequence of the asking price, not a property of the flat, so it belongs in the bid premium. The sold pages print days on market (`daysActive`, read in plan 02); candidates already carry `facts.daysOnMarket`. This plan adds the term to `fitBidPremium`'s cross-validation contest, exposes the premium's makeup through `expectedOverbidDetail`, refits the committed model and shows the result in the tracked-listing block. The table column that prints the premium for every row comes in plan 05.
Output: DOM term code + tests, refitted model and intermediate model, updated fit evidence.
</objective>
