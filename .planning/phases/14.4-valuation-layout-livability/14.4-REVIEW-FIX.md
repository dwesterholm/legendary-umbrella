## Did the refit move numbers

The refit is `scripts/fit-hedonic.ts` offline. The new model equals the old one in every field except `fittedAt` and the two new bounds on the days term (`lnLo` 0, `lnHi` 4,977, which is 144 days). Both fit logs are byte-identical to the old ones. So the coefficient, the intercepts, the CV numbers and the "kept" decision did not move.

What moved is the premium for listings older than 144 days. In the final replay of the 2026-10-13 snapshot:

- Folkungagatan 61 (4 days listed): premium still +20,2 %, profit still 1 123 047 kr. Its printed rank went 18 to 17 and its pre-rank 17 to 16, only because another flat dropped out of the list above it.
- Reimersholmsgatan 39 (353 days listed) is the flat the clamp hit: premium +2,9 % to +6,1 %, profit 1 160 872 to 1 049 938 kr, rank 15 to 24.
- The lower bound is 0 days (the 1st percentile is a flat listed 0 days), so the clamp does nothing at the fresh end. The review's point that a brand-new listing gets about 10 points more premium than an average one is not changed by this fix; that effect is the fitted coefficient itself.

Regenerated and committed in `1ac1308`: `data/hedonic-model.json`, `evidence/after/ranking.json`, `evidence/intermediate/ranking.json`, `evidence/valuation-report.{md,json}`, `evidence/final-replay.txt`. The intermediate model (`.planning/calibration/data/hedonic-model-14.4-intermediate.json`, not tracked) was refit too, because it also carries a days term and would no longer parse without the bounds.
