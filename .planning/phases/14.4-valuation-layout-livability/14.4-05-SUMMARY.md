# Phase 14.4 Plan 05: Credit ceiling removed, soft residual flag, Budpremie column Summary

**The 20 % credit ceiling is gone (profit takes the whole gap between value and expected price), replaced by a soft flag "ovanligt långt under modellpris" on a chosen basis that explains with numbers and never changes a figure; the table and mail gain "Skillnad" and "Budpremie" columns.**

## What this means for Daniel

Before, the profit never counted more than 20 % of a flat's renovated value as gain, whatever the gap. That was a hardcoded stand-in for "most cheap flats are cheap for reasons other than the kitchen". Now that the valuation itself contains avgift and association debt (plans 03 and 04), the stand-in is removed and every flat is credited with its full gap.

To keep the very large gaps visible, a flat that sits further below the model than the model's own typical error allows (more than two residual spreads, about 20,5 % under the model price per kvm) gets the flag "ovanligt långt under modellpris". The "Varför här" cell for such a row now carries a sentence with the numbers: how far below, the limit, the gap in kr, and which of avgift and association debt the valuation already contains. The flag only explains. It never moves a profit.

How many rows the flag marks on the 2026-10-13 snapshot (77 comparable rows):

| Basis | Rows flagged | What it compares |
|---|---|---|
| Expected final price against the model (default) | 10 of 77 | Like for like: the model is fitted on sold prices, so the price you are expected to pay is compared with it |
| Asking price against the model | 26 of 77 | Asking prices sit below sold prices by the expected overbid, so this fires on many ordinary flats |

All 10 rows flagged on the default basis were also among the 22 rows the old ceiling used to bind on. Switching basis is one constant (`RESIDUAL_FLAG_BASIS` in `src/lib/discovery/fit-score.ts`); every run prints both counts so the choice stays informed.

What the full gap did to the top 15 on this replay (same model, ceiling on versus off; plan 06 does the comparison against the frozen version-1 before):

- 10 of the 15 are the same listings. Dropped out: Folkungagatan 61 (9 to 18), Fridhemsgatan 41 (8 to 16), Karlsviksgatan 9 (14 to 21), Idungatan 5 (13 to 19), Tjurbergsgatan 38 (second listing, 15 to 23). Came in: Fyrverkarbacken 25 (23 to 8), Pokalvägen 4 (18 to 10), Fyrverkarbacken 23 (25 to 12), Ola Hanssonsgatan 4 (21 to 13), Reimersholmsgatan 39 (30 to 15).
- The top row is still Fatburs Kvarngata 18, but its profit goes from 1 285 476 kr to 2 318 010 kr. The sum of the top 15 profits goes from 17,4 M kr to 24,3 M kr, because the largest gaps are no longer clipped.
- 10 of the new top 15 carry the new flag. Those are the rows where the extra profit comes from a gap the features did not explain, so they are the ones to look at before believing the number.
- Folkungagatan 61 was never limited by the ceiling (its gap is 1 061 486 kr, under 20 % of its 5 503 113 kr value), so its profit stays 1 123 047 kr. Its rank falls from 9 to 18 of 77 only because flats above it now get their full gap. It sits 12,6 % below the model on the expected-final basis, inside the 20,5 % limit, so it is not flagged.
