# Phase 14.4 Plan 03: Avgift and BRF debt in the valuation Summary

**The hedonic model is now version 2 with nine features: a flat's avgift per kvm and its association's debt per kvm lower the predicted price (-0,64 % per 10 kr/kvm/mån, -0,24 % per 1 000 kr/kvm, both with t near 3), tomträtt is recorded as not estimable, and no further sold pages need reading.**

## What this means for Daniel

Your earlier point was that high avgift and BRF debt should be in the valuation and not a 20 % ceiling. That is now done on the model side. The 20 % ceiling itself is still in place; plan 05 removes it.

What the model learned from the 1 200 sold flats that have the data:

- **Avgift.** 10 kr per kvm and month more avgift lowers the price per kvm by about 0,64 % (error margin 0,2 percentage points, t = -3,3). A 40 kvm flat paying 1 000 kr/mån more avgift is 25 kr/kvm/mån higher, so about 1,6 % cheaper per kvm.
- **BRF debt.** 1 000 kr/kvm more debt lowers the price per kvm by about 0,24 % (error margin 0,08 percentage points, t = -2,9). A flat in an association with 14 700 kr/kvm of debt, against the average of 5 600, comes out about 2,2 % lower.
- **Tomträtt.** Cannot be estimated. All 1 155 sold pages that state a tenure say "Bostadsrätt", so there is nothing to compare. The model records it as zero with the reason "saknas i underlaget". The one "Äganderätt" row (Igeldammsgatan 5, a 43 kvm property with no avgift) and the 44 rows without a tenure text are treated as unknown, not as tomträtt.
- **Neither guard fired.** The library's sign and stability check left both coefficients alone, so nothing was zeroed or replaced.

The new features barely change how well the model predicts sold prices. R² goes from 0,6095 to 0,6113 and the cross-validation error (how wrong it is on sales it did not train on) stays at 0,115 in log terms, about ±12 % per kvm. The value is in individual flats with unusual avgift or debt, not in a tighter overall fit.

## The bigger mover is not the new features

The research (Pitfall 6) warned that the refit would move predictions for reasons other than the new features. It does. The 1 200 enriched rows let the second stage (balcony and build year) switch from the study's values to values fitted on the data:

| | Version-1 model | Old six features on today's rows | New nine-feature model |
|---|---|---|---|
| Balcony | +1,9 % (study value) | +8,0 % (fitted, at the 8 % cap) | +8,0 % (at the cap) |
| Built before 1931 | no adjustment | +4,2 % | +3,9 % |
| 1931-1960 | no adjustment | -3,1 % | -2,9 % |
| 1961-1990 | no adjustment | -8,0 % (at the cap) | -8,0 % (at the cap) |
| 1991 or later | no adjustment | -6,3 % | -4,5 % |

The first six coefficients (neighbour level, floor, ground floor, size, time trend, distance to centre) are identical between the version-1 model and the old-six-features model: same n of 7 380, same R² and CV. So everything that changed there comes from stage 2.

Folkungagatan 61 shows the size of it. Its model resale value was 5 006 594 kr before, 5 527 830 kr with only the stage-2 change (+10,4 %), and 5 503 113 kr with the new features on top (-0,45 %). Its rank in the replay went from 29 to 8. Almost all of that is the balcony and build-year switch, not avgift or debt. Folkungagatan 61's avgift is known (2 232 kr, 63,8 kr/kvm/mån against a mean of 61,1) and its BRF debt is unknown, so the table names debt as imputed.

Two of the five stage-2 numbers sit exactly on the 8 % limit the library puts on balcony and build-year effects. That means the data wanted more than 8 %, and the limit decided the value. Nothing was changed because the rule is measured, not tuned, but a +8 % balcony premium against a +1,9 % study value is a large step and deserves a look before plan 06's report is read as a verdict on avgift and debt.

## R-06: no more pages

The rule says to read the remaining ≈ 4 500 sold pages only if a new coefficient is within 2 standard errors of zero. Both are beyond it (t = -3,3 and -2,9). Even if every flat in an association shared one identical error, the debt t-value would still be about -2,3. Nothing more is read. Details in `evidence/r06-decision.md`.
