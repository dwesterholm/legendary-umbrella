## Plain-words result (for Daniel)

**What changed in the weekly table.** Two new columns sit after the sun hours. Korsdrag says whether the windows are on two opposite sides, so air can pass through (ja / nej / okänd). Sovrum says whether the bedroom faces the quiet courtyard side (gård) or the street (gata). A flat where the read could not say shows "okänd" and gets no bonus and no penalty. A flat that does have korsdrag gets 1 % of its resale value added to its taste score, and a courtyard bedroom another 1 %. Taste is still capped at 5 % of resale in total, so neither can lift a bad deal. No flat in the live run reached that cap (0 of 75).

**This is taste, not market value.** Buyers probably pay more for both, but no sold flat in our data carries a floor plan, so the valuation model cannot know it. Nothing here changes what a flat is estimated to be worth. It only changes how Daniel's own preference orders the list.

**How often the read gave an answer (75 flats in the live run, 74 got a layout; one flat had no usable plan picture).** After the confidence gate (0,6, plus photo evidence for street or courtyard):

| Field | Usable answers | Of 75 |
|---|---|---|
| Balcony (street, courtyard or confidently none) | 41 (27 of them street or courtyard) | 55 % |
| Window sides (korsdrag known) | 40 | 53 % |
| Bedroom side | 13 | 17 % |

For 25 of the 75 flats the sun score now uses the balcony side from the plan instead of the half-weight guess from 14.3 (33 %). The reads by confidence: 8 under 0,5; 25 between 0,5 and 0,6 (read but kept out by the gate); 13 between 0,6 and 0,7; 28 at 0,7 or above. So a third of all reads sit just under the line.

**Agreement with the footprint.** Only 2 flats could be compared (the listing states a facade, the footprint is known, and the balcony side was usable). Both agree (courtyard = west in both). With n = 2 this says nothing about the rate. Internal consistency (bedroom said to be on the balcony side but believed on the other side): 0 contradictions in 3 testable flats.

**By-eye check of 12 plans** (full table in `evidence/layout-eye-check.md`, I read each floor plan and photos myself; I could not see the broker photos the read also used):

- Balcony: 7 right, 1 wrong, 4 could not be judged. Whether there is a balcony at all matched Booli's own balcony flag in 12 of 12. Street versus courtyard could be confirmed in 1 flat, refuted in 1, and left open in 4 because the photos show neither a road nor a courtyard floor.
- Window sides: 8 right, 3 wrong, 1 not judgeable. The wrong ones: the read said two opposite sides where the plan has windows on one wall only (142942 and 324812, where interior glass between living room and sleeping alcove was probably taken for a window), and said one side where the plan has windows at both ends (324847).
- Bedroom side: 0 confirmed, 1 wrong, 11 not judgeable (mostly "okänd" at the gate, which is the honest answer).
- The gate let through "korsdrag ja" for 3 of the 12; 2 are wrong (at confidence 0,60 and 0,70), 1 is right. Across the whole run 12 flats got "korsdrag ja". If the rate in my sample holds, most of those 12 bonuses are wrong. A wrong bonus is 1 % of resale, roughly 40 000 to 60 000 kr on these flats. "Korsdrag nej" was let through for 4 of the 12 and all 4 are right.

**Folkungagatan 61 (Booli 150148), live.** The read says courtyard for both balcony and bedroom at confidence 0,55, the same wrong answer as the plan 07 smoke. The evidence it gave is "courtyard greenery"; the green in the pictures is artificial grass on the balcony floor. The 0,6 gate kept it out: the live table shows Korsdrag okänd and Sovrum okänd, the Smak cell is 166 561 kr (sun 1,0 %, hub 2,0 %, korsdrag 0,0 %, tyst sovrum 0,0 %), so the layout changed its taste by nothing. The fixture replay with the hand-written truth (balcony street, bedroom street, windows one side) prints nej and gata, also with no change in taste.

**Money.** Layout reads: 73 calls and 1 from the cache, 7,89 kr this run (cap 40 kr, never close), 8,00 kr lifetime including the plan 07 smoke. That is about 0,11 kr per flat, far under the 0,43 kr the research assumed. Picture reads: 75 calls, 35,36 kr, none from cache. The picture cache did not hit because the code review fix WR-05 (14.2, 15:14 on 2026-10-13) changed the cache key after the old cache was written, so all 75 were bought again once; they are cached now and the next run should cost about 0 kr. Apify: 0 USD (local browser, 224 pages). Total of the run: about 43 kr.
