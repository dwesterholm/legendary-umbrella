# Phase 14.4 Plan 02: Sold-page enrichment (v2) Summary

**The sold-price cache now stores avgift, BRF id, tenure, days on market, first price and photo ids, proven on real pages through the free local browser, but the 1 200-row read stopped itself after 50 pages and only 52 rows carry the new data.**

## What this means for Daniel

The code part is done and works. Reading real Booli pages needs no paid service: 55 sold pages and 5 BRF pages loaded in about 1,5 to 2,2 seconds each, with no block page from Booli and no failed page load.

The data part is not done. The safety rule you set (stop if 5 % of the first 50 pages fail) tripped at exactly 3 of 50 (6,0 %). The three were not blocks. Booli simply returned a page with no sold-flat data on it. Two of them were sales from 2025-10-08, which are now just over a year old; Booli's sold pages lose their details at about that age (we saw the same in 14.1). The third was a sale from 2026-02-07 and I do not know why it came back empty.

I did not re-run or loosen the rule, because the plan says no same-day retry and the rule is your call. Plan 03 would fit the model on 52 rows, which is far too few (the research wanted about 1 200), so I marked this plan `halted`. That makes the orchestrator report plan 03 as blocked instead of fitting on almost nothing.

Choices for continuing, my recommendation first:

1. Run the same command tomorrow with `--enrich-since 2025-10-20` instead of 2025-10-01. That removes most pages near the one-year edge. The 3 failed rows are already in the ledger with one attempt each and will be skipped after one more failure. The rule stays as you set it. Even then, a 2 % real failure rate trips a 50-page check about 1 time in 13, so a second stop is possible and is not a block.
2. Keep the date and ask the rule to count only after 100 attempts. That needs your approval because D-04 is your rule.
3. Accept a small sample. Not recommended: the model's three new coefficients would be too uncertain.

### Auto-fixed Issues

**1. [Rule 1 - Bug] Stratified draw made fixed so a resume does not extend the sample**
- **Found during:** Task 2 (designing the resume path for task 3)

- **Fix:** With `--stratify` the draw covers every row of the window (finished ones included) and finished rows are dropped afterwards. A unit test pins it.
