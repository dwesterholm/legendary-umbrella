<objective>
Judge the second half of the pool the same blind way, then fix the truth set, its dev/holdout split and its read order in one locked commit before any paid call (LAYACC-01, R-05).

Purpose: the switch decision is only honest if nobody can choose the flats or the split after seeing how a variant did. Locking first, with a hash of the rows, makes that checkable. The lock also tells, before a krona is spent, whether each field has the 12 decided truth rows R-05 requires; a field that does not is "not measurable" and the switch stays off whatever the rounds show.
Output: a locked truth/layout-truth.json.
</objective>
