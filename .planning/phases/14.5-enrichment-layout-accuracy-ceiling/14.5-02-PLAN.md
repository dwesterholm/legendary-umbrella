<objective>
Build the measuring stick for the layout read before anyone judges a flat or pays for a read: the truth-file format, per-field accuracy, the confidence curve, the gate chosen from it, the switch verdict with its stop rules, and the free tools the truth-set plans use (worklist, check, select-and-lock). LAYACC-01 (format and tools), LAYACC-02 (measurement), LAYACC-03 (the decision rule), per D-09, D-11, D-12 as amended by R-05.

Purpose: Daniel said "fix the layout misreads, don't continue until it's at least > 75 % reliable". Reliable has to be measured on flats whose truth is known and decided by a rule written before the numbers exist, otherwise a lucky prompt or a convenient threshold decides. This plan writes that rule into code: precision of the answers that move money, a gate taken from the measured curve on development rows and checked on locked holdout rows, at least 12 decided truth rows per field, Wilson intervals printed so Daniel sees how little 20 flats can prove.
Output: a pure module, one pure function in layout-fact.ts, a free runner script and a three-row fixture. No paid call, no Booli page.
</objective>
