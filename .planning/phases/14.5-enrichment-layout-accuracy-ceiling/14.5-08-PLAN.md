<objective>
Run the first two layout experiments of D-10 on the locked truth set, at the true price: (a) today's read as the baseline, (b) the read with explicit evidence rules and a certainty per field, both on Sonnet with the same four Booli pictures (LAYACC-02).

Purpose: Daniel wants the layout read fixed to at least 75 % before it influences anything. The baseline shows how far today's read is from that, field by field; (b) shows whether writing the evidence rules into the prompt (cars and shopfronts mean street, bicycle racks and lawns mean courtyard, artificial grass is not greenery, the entrance arrow is not a side) closes the gap. Each round is capped at 40 kr and cached, so a repeat costs nothing.
Output: one live schema smoke, two rounds with their accuracy reports, and the round decision so far.
</objective>
