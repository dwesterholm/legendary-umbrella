## Deviations from Plan

### Auto-fixed Issues

**1. [Rule 1 - Bug] The plan's stage-2 CV formula contradicts the plan's own expected result**
- **Found during:** Task 3 (first test run)
- **Issue:** The plan scores a held-out row as its residual minus ln(1 + balcony pct if balcony, else 0) minus ln(1 + bucket pct), "as the model applies them". On a synthetic set with a true +11 % balcony effect that gave a HIGHER error at ±12 % than at ±8 %, the opposite of the plan's test. Cause: the build-year bucket values are levels at the average balcony mix (fitStage2 adds gamma x share into them), so adding the full balcony pct on top makes every flat with a known balcony flag come out about (balcony share x balcony effect) too high. A bigger balcony effect is then penalised for that bias, not for predicting worse.
- **Fix:** `stage2CvRmse` returns three numbers: `without`, `withCap` (the regression's own prediction, balcony centred on the training share of balconies; this is what the cap decision reads) and `withCapAsApplied` (the production multipliers). Both appear in the fit log and the report JSON (`stage2.cv.byCap` and `stage2.cv.asApplied`). Tests pin both behaviours.
- **Files modified:** `src/lib/discovery/hedonic-model.ts`, `hedonic-model.test.ts`, `scripts/fit-hedonic.ts`
- **Commit:** `562d7fe`

**2. [Rule 2 - Missing functionality] valuation-report model block carries the cap**
- **Found during:** Task 2
- **Issue:** The narrative printed the cap from the constant; to print the model's own cap the report object needed the value.
- **Fix:** `ValuationReport.model.stage2CapPct` added (filled from `modelStage2Cap(afterModel)`).
- **Commit:** `65913e0`

**Total deviations:** 2 auto-fixed (1 bug in the plan's formula, 1 missing field). **Impact:** none on scope. The first one changes which number is the cap verdict's input, and on the real data both measures agree on the direction (see below).

## Open Points for the Orchestrator and Daniel

- **Production over-values flats with a known balcony flag.** `predictKrPerSqm` multiplies by (1 + balcony pct) for a known balcony and by 1 otherwise, while the build-year values already include the average balcony effect. With 1 845 of 3 634 detail rows having a balcony (51 %) and a balcony effect of +8 %, flats with a known balcony flag are priced about 4 % too high, whether they have a balcony or not; flats with the flag unknown are unbiased. I did not change this (it alters how the model prices every candidate, an architectural call); the as-applied CV line makes it visible. Worth a decision in plan 11 or 12.
- **The cap verdict is close.** ±12 % beats ±8 % by 0,4 % of the error on the centred measure and by 0,6 % as applied (0,10971 against 0,11038). Both point to ±12 %, but a larger gain would be easier to defend. The 1961-1990 bucket (raw -11,2 %) is the value that really needs the room; the balcony (raw +8,3 %) barely exceeds ±8 %.
