## What it means

Daniel's rule was that the layout read must not move any ranking until it is right at least 75 % of the time when it commits to an answer, for all three things it reads. The five variants were tested on 60 flats judged by hand. Result per field:

- **Balcony side (street or courtyard):** passes. Opus (c) was right on 6 of 6 committed holdout answers, the two-step read (d) on 5 of 5, Jev (e) on 4 of 5. There are only 14 flats with a judgeable balcony, so even a perfect 6 of 6 only proves "at least 61 %" with 95 % confidence.
- **Korsdrag (windows on two opposite sides, a through-draught):** fails in every variant. The rule needs 5 committed "ja" on the development half to pick a threshold. The truth set has 4 flats that truly have this (3 on development, 1 on holdout), so a reader that is right every time can give at most 3. Opus got 3 of 4 at its lowest threshold, one answer short.
- **Bedroom side:** cannot be measured. 9 flats have a judgeable bedroom side, 12 are needed. Raw, Opus was right 8 of 8 and the two-step read 6 of 6, which is promising and unprovable here.

Because the rule asks for one variant that passes all three, there is no winner, so nothing changes in production: the Sol, Korsdrag and Sovrum cells stay "okänd", the taste bonus and the sun hours ignore the layout, and the weekly job keeps reading and saving layouts so the measurement can continue.

## Two options for korsdrag (for Daniel)

1. **More flats in the truth set.** Add flats with windows on two opposite sides (estimate: 8 to 10 in total, since about half land on the development half; not measured), lock the set again, and read the new flats with each variant. Cost: hand-judging time and a fresh lock.
2. **Change what the 12-row minimum counts.** Today it counts all 59 decided flats, so the minimum is met on paper while only 4 are real "ja". Counting true "ja" flats instead would make korsdrag not measurable until more exist, which is the same situation as bedroom side. This changes the measure itself, so it should be decided before looking at which variant would then pass.

A third question sits next to these: whether to allow a balcony-only switch. That is also a rule change (D-11 asks for all three fields) and was not made.
