# Phase 14.5 Plan 11: Refit, cap decision and the balcony double count Summary

**The price model is refitted on 3 912 enriched sales with the stage-2 guard widened once to +-12 % by the written rule (error 0,10188 against 0,10225), and production pricing no longer counts the average balcony effect twice, which lowers every resale value by 4,0 % and moves Folkungagatan 61 from plats 17 to 16 with profit down 184 386 kr.**

## Accomplishments

### What the data now says (3 912 enriched sales; the first model had about 1 200)

- **Avgift (monthly fee per kvm):** 10 kr more per kvm and month is -0,64 % on the price per kvm in 14.4 (SE 0,20, t 3,3) and -1,00 % now (SE 0,11, t 8,9). The estimate grew by half and the uncertainty halved.
- **BRF debt per kvm:** 1 000 kr more is -0,24 % in 14.4 (SE 0,08, t 2,9) and -0,25 % now (SE 0,05, t 4,7). Same size, much more certain. 2 329 of the 3 912 enriched rows carry a debt figure (649 associations). Avgift and debt are correlated 0,30 (n 2 320), VIF 1,22 and 1,17, so they are separable.
- **Other stage-1 terms barely moved** (floor +1,6 % per floor, ground floor -6,4 %, neighbour level 1,066 -> 1,050, area 0,0447 -> 0,0404, centrality -0,0071 -> -0,0044 with SE 0,0042 both times). R2 0,611 -> 0,616, cvRmseLog 0,1151 -> 0,1143. Tomträtt cannot be estimated. Ceiling height: 0 sold rows state a height, not fitted.
- **Stage 2 (3 615 rows with balcony and build year), raw values with approximate SE:** balcony +8,3 % (0,3); built before 1931 +3,6 % (0,2); 1931-1960 -2,2 % (0,4); 1961-1990 -11,2 % (0,7, n 256); 1991 and later -5,3 % (0,6). 14.4 did not compute SE for stage 2.
- **The guard still bound at +-8 %:** balcony (+8,3 against 8,0) and 1961-1990 (-11,2 against -8,0). Balcony barely exceeds the limit; the build-year bucket is the value that needed room. At +-12 % nothing sits on the limit.

### The cap decision (R-01)

Error on held-out sales, in ln kr/kvm: no stage 2 0,11557; +-8 % 0,10225; +-12 % 0,10188. By the rule (+-12 % if not worse) +-12 % is committed. The gain over +-8 % is 0,00037, about 0,4 % of the error, which is thin; I did not run a significance test. The older measure that applies the uncentred balcony gives 0,11038 against 0,10971 (gain 0,00067), same direction. Stage-1 cross-validation cannot separate the caps (0,11433 for both). The choice did not look at Folkungagatan 61. No second widening.

### The double count fix (orchestrator addendum)

The build-year values are levels for a flat at the average balcony mix (51 % of the sample, `share` 0,509), so adding the full balcony percentage on top counted the average balcony effect twice. `predictKrPerSqm` now multiplies by (1 + pct)^(balcony - share), the form the fit and the stage-2 cross-validation use. A flat with a balcony gets (1 + pct)^(0,49), one without (1 + pct)^(-0,51), an unknown flag is unchanged. Effect on the replay (cap +-12 %, same model, snapshot 2026-10-13):

- **Every one of the 77 ranked flats has a known balcony flag, so every resale value fell by the same 3,99 %** (1,0832^-0,509). Rank changed for 35 of 77 flats, the most for Tideliusgatan 55 (63 -> 68), because profit is a small gap between resale and the expected price and a flat 4 % shift hits low-gap flats hardest.
- The median of expected final price over model price (the model-health line) moved from 0,921 to 0,954, closer to 1 and inside the 0,85-1,15 band either way.
- The top 15 keeps the same 14 flats; Fyrverkarbacken 25 and Hornsgatan 119 swap places (9/10) and Ringvägen 162 replaces Folkungagatan 61 at 15.
- **Folkungagatan 61: the fix lowers it.** Rank 15 -> 16, resale 5 508 047 -> 5 288 313 kr (-219 734), profit 1 144 996 -> 938 661 kr (-206 335).
