# Phase 14.5 Plan 01: Ceiling-height parser and Takhöjd column Summary

**Pure Swedish parser that reads a stated ceiling height (2,2 to 5,0 m) out of broker text, stores it as a nullable CandidateFacts number, and shows it in a new "Takhöjd" column of the weekly fit table**

## Performance

- **Duration:** 9 min
- **Started:** 2026-10-17T13:42:00Z
- **Completed:** 2026-10-17T13:51:00Z
- **Tasks:** 2
- **Files modified:** 12 (2 created, 10 modified)

## Accomplishments

- `ceilingHeightFromText` reads "takhöjd 3,2 m", "takhöjd på ca 3 meter", "3,5 meters takhöjd", "tre meter i takhöjd", "takhöjd 270 cm" and the other phrasings; it rejects low ceilings, heights of cellars, storage rooms, attics, garages and similar spaces in the same clause, areas ("2,5 m2"), room counts and anything outside 2,2 to 5,0 m. 63 inline test cases, 6 more across watch-enrich, fit-score and fit-pipeline.
- "högt i tak", "höga tak", "generös takhöjd" and kin set `tallCeilingPhrase` true and never become a number.
- The three facts flow `watch-enrich` (next to the sun phrases, before the text is dropped) to `row.facts` to `FitScoreResult` (comparable and not-comparable branch) to the table cell. Every stored snapshot has no text, so its rows show "okänd"; the replay record is unchanged apart from the new column.
- No picture-based height estimate exists anywhere (D-08) and nothing touches the sold side (R-03).
