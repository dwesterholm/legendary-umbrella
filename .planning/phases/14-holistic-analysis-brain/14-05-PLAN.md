<objective>
Wire real renovated-vs-unrenovated area comps into every enriched discovery candidate (ANL-02) and
make comps spend share the single `CAP_VISION_SEK_MAX` budget pool with the vision pass (D-14-08).

Purpose: `computeAreaComps` has been built and tested since 2026-07-10 but nothing ever calls it on
the discovery path — R_med/U_med never reach a candidate, which is why the analysis is image-only
(SPEC defect D3). Every candidate already carries `areaLabel` (Booli's own `descriptiveAreaName`),
which resolves through the SAME cached `resolveArea` the job already uses, so no `runVisionForJob`
signature change is needed (D-14-11). Comps are per-AREA, so they must be fetched once per area and
reused — double-counting them per candidate would be real money.

Output: `renderSek`/`estimateCompsFetchSek`/`estimateBrfLookupSek` in `cost.ts`, an
additive-optional `initialSpentSek` on `runVisionPass`, and `resolveCompsForCandidates` in `job.ts`
wired into `runVisionForJob`. Every candidate that can get comps gets `areaComps`; nothing else in
`runVisionForJob`'s persistence contract changes.
</objective>
