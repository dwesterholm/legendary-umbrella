<objective>
Gap closure for 14-VERIFICATION.md's ANL-03/ANL-04 failures — the DATA SPINE half.

Close **CR-02 (part 1 of 2)**: `scoreExtraction` returns `{ normalized, grade, perFieldConfidence }`
and `brf-lookup.ts:137` keeps only `normalized`, throwing away the sanity-band confidence downgrade
(`src/lib/brf/sanity.ts` forces confidence to 0.2 — below `OSAKER_THRESHOLD = 0.5` — when
`skuldPerKvm`/`avgiftsniva` falls outside its plausible band, deliberately WITHOUT dropping the
value). This plan carries that signal through onto `BrfSummary` and publishes the single gate
function every consumer must use. Plan 14-10 (wave 2) then applies the gate to display and to the
debt-inclusive discount math — that is what makes ANL-04 verifiable again.

Close **CR-04**: `lookupBrfSummary`'s catch block reports `costSek: 0` for extractions Anthropic
already billed (`extract.ts` throws `CLAUDE_REFUSAL`/`CLAUDE_PARSE_EMPTY` after 1 billed call and
`CLAUDE_MAX_TOKENS` after 2), so the shared `CAP_VISION_SEK_MAX = 10` pool seeded via
`initialSpentSek` (D-14-08) can be exceeded by ~60%.

Purpose: `BrfSummary` is the only shape that crosses the `brf-lookup.ts` boundary. If the confidence
signal is not on it, no downstream consumer can be honest — the display asserts a garbage figure as
fact and the ANL-04 normalization silently misclassifies. Fixing the shape first (wave 1) lets the
consumer fixes (wave 2) be a pure application of one named predicate.

Output: `BrfSummary.fieldConfidence`, `brfFieldTrusted`, a legacy-tolerant `brfSummarySchema`,
`BILLED_CALLS_BY_EXTRACTION_CODE`, and the corrected `job.ts` accumulation comment.
</objective>
