Three confirmed, source-level data-correctness defects, all in `confounder-guard.ts`:

- **CR-02 (part 2 of 2)** — `normalizeForConfounders` rule 1 trusts `brf.skuldPerKvm` behind only a
  `Number.isFinite` check (lines 145-155), and `buildBrfItem` prints it verbatim (lines 382-385). A
  classic misextraction (total debt read as debt/m²) is therefore shown as a normal reading AND
  pushed into `effectivePricePerSqm = pricePerSqm + brf.skuldPerKvm`, where it can flip
  `deepDiscount` from true to false and silently disable the SPEC §2.6 20% attribution cap — the
  exact guard ANL-04 exists to enforce. **This task is what makes ANL-04 verifiable again.**
- **CR-01** — line 381 prints `avgiftsniva` as `kr/mån`. The field is unambiguously årsavgift per
  kvm, SEK/m² och ÅR (`src/lib/brf/prompt.ts:29`, `src/lib/brf/sanity.ts:27` band 300-1200,
  `src/lib/brf/score.ts:15`). A real in-band 650 renders as "650 kr/mån" when the true monthly fee
  for a 70 m² flat is ~3 792 kr/mån — a ~6x understatement of a financial figure, on the one
  surface framed as "trust this, it's data not image interpretation".
- **CR-03** — line 387 concatenates the raw enum (`"planerat" | "nyligen_genomfort" | "ej_nämnt"`)
  into Swedish prose. `"ej_nämnt"` means the document does not mention stambyte at all
  (`src/lib/brf/prompt.ts:40`), so every BRF-bearing candidate whose document is silent renders
  "Stambyte-läge: ej_nämnt." — an absence of information presented as an information item, against
  ANL-01's "≥1 **actionable** item" bar.

SEMANTICS of the data being composed and priced. These three fixes are that semantics.

Output: a confidence-gated `normalizeForConfounders`, a unit-correct and confidence-gated
`buildBrfItem`, an enum-to-prose map that suppresses absence-of-information, and a test suite that
proves each defect cannot return.
