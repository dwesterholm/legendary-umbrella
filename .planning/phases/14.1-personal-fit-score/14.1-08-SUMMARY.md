# Phase 14.1 Plan 08: Real hedonic model Summary

**The price model is now fitted on the 7 380 cleaned sold flats and committed as `data/hedonic-model.json`: it explains 61 % of price differences and is off by about 12 % on flats it has not seen. The 35 sold-detail pages bought for 4.23 USD were too few and too one-sided to measure balcony or build year, so those two stay at the study's value.**

## What this plan did, in plain terms

The ranking needs a number for "what should a flat like this sell for". This plan produced that number's formula from real Stockholm sales and saved it in a file the ranking script loads. Two things came with it. First, an attempt to buy extra facts (build year, balcony) for a sample of sold flats from Booli's detail pages. Second, a check on the odd result from plan 05, where the model said bigger flats cost more per square metre.

## Accomplishments

- **Model file committed:** `data/hedonic-model.json`, 7 380 sales, R2 0.606, cross-validated error 0.116 in log scale (about 12 %), fitted size range 35 to 120 m2. Both sanity thresholds pass (R2 at least 0.35, CV-RMSE at most 0.13). Nothing was tuned to pass.
- **Offline refit proven:** `npx tsx scripts/fit-hedonic.ts` reads only the local cache, imports nothing from the Booli transport or client, and runs in about 10 seconds. It excluded the one Folkungagatan 61 row (35 m2) from training, as the plan requires.
- **Sold-detail enrichment built and tested:** it reads build year, balcony and list price from a detail page. All three saved probe pages parse to exactly the values written down in 14.1-PROBES.md (1908 / 1933 / none, balcony yes only for Ola Hanssonsgatan 6, list price 3 595 000 / none / 3 195 000).
- **Size question answered with data** (below).
- **Replay with the real model:** Folkungagatan 61 comes out 26th of 46 overall and 18th of 34 in Södermalm, model price 132 614 kr/m2, expected overbid 15.9 %, expected sale price 4 280 931 kr.

## The size coefficient, and what Daniel is asked to confirm

Plan 05 found that bigger flats cost more per square metre in this data, which is the wrong way round for a normal price model, so the direction rule (D-18) zeroed it. The suspicion was that this came from a tangle between size and time: the 60 to 120 m2 flats in the cache are all recent. I tested that two ways, both offline and free:

1. Size as separate bands against 35-40 m2 instead of one slope, with the market-trend term in the model.
2. The same thing on the latest three months only (sales since 2026-07-12), where all six size bands sit side by side, so time cannot be the explanation.

| Storleksband | Alla försäljningar | Sedan 2026-07-12 |
|---|---|---|
| 40-45 kvm | -0,2 % (SE 0,4) | +0,9 % (SE 1,1) |
| 45-50 kvm | -1,1 % (SE 0,5) | -0,1 % (SE 1,2) |
| 50-60 kvm | +1,0 % (SE 0,4) | +1,8 % (SE 1,1) |
| 60-80 kvm | +2,8 % (SE 0,6) | +3,4 % (SE 1,0) |
| 80-120 kvm | +4,4 % (SE 0,6) | +4,9 % (SE 1,0) |

The result is the same in both: the effect is real and stable (t about 8), not noise and not a time artefact. Between 35 and 50 m2, the range that fits a 4 M kr budget in central Stockholm, the difference is about zero. Above 60 m2, flats sell for 3 to 5 % more per m2.

**What I did:** kept the rule. The size effect is set to zero, and the model file's own note says why in Swedish. The cost: for flats of 60 to 120 m2 the model prices about 5 % too low; for the 35 to 50 m2 flats Daniel is looking at it makes no difference. **Daniel decides** whether to drop the direction rule for size (a one-line change in `EXPECTED_SIGNS` in `hedonic-model.ts`, then re-run the free fit). I did not change it because the instruction was to keep the guard and flag the choice.

## Sold-detail enrichment: what it cost and what it gave

**Path A was taken.** PROBES keys that decided it: `SOLD_DETAIL_BUILD_YEAR: present`, `SOLD_DETAIL_BALCONY: present`, `SOLD_DETAIL_LIST_PRICE: present`.

| | |
|---|---|
| Real Apify spend (runs API, since 23:20 UTC) | **4.2292 USD** (about 46.5 SEK), 80 renders in two batches (2.134 and 2.066 USD printed at the time, 0.053 USD per render) |
| Cap given for this plan | 4.50 USD (headroom check before the first paid render: 11.71 USD) |
| Headroom left | **7.47 USD** (plans 09 and 10 need about 6 USD) |
| Pages enriched | **35** of 80 attempts (plan asked for 150-300; the cap allows about 80 renders) |
| Of the 35 | build year on 32, balcony listed on 15, list price on 18, sold 2025-10-14 to 2026-10-11 |

Why the yield was low (counts from the two batches' logs):

- **Sales from before October 2025: 0 of 15 attempts worked** (12 pages with no sold object, 3 timeouts). Booli appears to stop showing the sold object after about a year. After batch 1 showed this, I limited batch 2 to sales from 2025-10-01 on (`--enrich-since`).
- **Sales from October 2025 on: 35 of 65 attempts worked** (54 %); 11 had no sold object and 19 timed out. Timeouts are billed too.

Why the 35 pages are not in the model: stage 2 needs build-year groups to be measurable. Of the 32 pages with both facts, 25 are buildings from before 1931, and the other three age groups have 4, 1 and 2 pages. The data says balcony +8,0 % (the cap) and build-year effects from -8 % to +4 %, but with 1 to 4 pages per group those numbers are noise. Left in, the library would have printed them as "fit" because it only asks for 30 rows in total. So the fit script requires at least 20 sales per build-year group and otherwise refits without the detail sample; the measured values are printed for information only. Result: balcony = the study's +1,9 % (marked "studie" in the model), build year absent. This is the plan's own fallback path (D-18, R-05), reached by the sample being too small rather than by Booli not having the data.

## Decisions Made

See `key-decisions` above. Two smaller ones:

- An empty `amenities` list is read as "balcony not listed" (false), not unknown. PROBES notes it cannot tell "none" from "not listed", but a for-sale card carries the same list, so the model learns what a card can show. No amenities field at all is null, and a page with all three facts null is counted as a failed render, not as "no balcony".
- The page function returns only the flight text (not the Apollo transport as the probe dump did), which halves the dataset item.

## Deviations from Plan

**1. [Rule 1 - Bug] Plan said to render `row.url`; 61 % of cached urls are /bostad/ residence pages**
- **Found during:** Task 1, reading the cache before spending
- **Issue:** 4 485 of 7 431 rows have `/bostad/<residenceId>`, a different page type that was never probed.
- **Fix:** `soldDetailUrl(booliId)` builds `https://www.booli.se/annons/<digits>` (the shape probed in 14.1-02); the script also checks `isBooliUrl`. Test covers a `/bostad/` row.
- **Commit:** a78b341

**2. [Rule 1 - Bug] Old sales return no data, and paid renders were wasted on them**
- **Found during:** Task 1, first paid batch (40 renders, 14 enriched)
- **Issue:** 12 of 12 non-timeout attempts on sales from before October 2025 had no sold object. They had cost real money.
- **Fix:** `selectEnrichmentSample(..., { soldSince })` and `--enrich-since`; batch 2 used 2025-10-01. Choosing by sale date selects on a model feature, so it does not bias balcony or build-year effects (those are fitted on what is left after the time trend).
- **Commit:** 7980c19

**3. [Plan target missed, budget-driven] 35 enriched rows instead of 150-300**
- The orchestrator's hard cap (4.5 USD) and the real cost (0.053 USD per render, 54 % yield on usable sales) allow about 80 renders. The acceptance line "between 150 and 300 rows with `enriched` set" is not met. A bigger sample would need about 3 USD per 30 more usable pages, and only sales from the last year can be enriched, so the ceiling is the number of sales in the last year that Booli still shows.

**4. [Rule 2 - Missing critical] Gate against a thin detail sample in `fit-hedonic.ts`**
- **Found during:** Task 2, reading the first fit
- **Issue:** the library accepts any detail sample of 30 rows or more and would have stored build-year effects fitted on 1 to 4 pages per group as "source: fit", moving candidate prices by up to 8 % on noise.
- **Fix:** the script needs 20 sales in each build-year group (`STAGE2_MIN_PER_BUCKET`), otherwise it refits without the detail sample, prints the measured values for information, and puts the reason in the model's note and fallback lines. This is a script-level rule, not a change to the model library. It is not tuned to pass the R2/CV sanity check.
- **Commit:** d1b5b6f

**5. [Plan divergence] Two paid batches instead of one command** (a 40-render test batch to read the real cost per render, then 40 more). Planned total spend of 7-12 USD was replaced by the 4.5 USD cap.

**Total deviations:** 3 auto-fixed (2 bug, 1 missing critical), 1 budget-driven miss of an acceptance number, 1 procedural.
**Impact on plan:** the model file, the offline refit and the plain-language report are delivered as specified. Balcony and build year are at the documented fallback, not measured.
