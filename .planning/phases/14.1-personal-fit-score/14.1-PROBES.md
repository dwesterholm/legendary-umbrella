How to read each value (evidence is in the output below):

- **SOLD_DETAIL_BUILD_YEAR: present.** The sold detail flight carries `constructionYear` on the SoldProperty entity (1908 for Skeppargatan 39, 1933 for Ola Hanssonsgatan 6). It is NOT in an InfoPoint. Coverage 2 of 3 pages: Tantogatan 59 had none, so expect roughly a third of sold flats to lack it.
- **SOLD_DETAIL_BALCONY: present.** The sold entity has an `amenities` array. Ola Hanssonsgatan 6 lists `elevator, balcony`; the other two have an empty array. An empty array cannot tell "no balcony" from "not listed", the same ambiguity the for-sale cards have. Coverage of positive balcony hits was 1 of 3.
- **SOLD_DETAIL_LIST_PRICE: present.** `listPrice.raw` (asking, FormattedValue) on 2 of 3 pages (3 595 000 and 3 195 000); missing on Skeppargatan 39. For that page the asking price can still be recovered from the `priceDiff` InfoPoint or the card label.
- **SLUTPRIS_PAGE_DEPTH: 30.** Page 30 returned 35 sold cards. Pages 10 and 60 each returned an empty page (the actor run ended SUCCEEDED at 245 s with no Apollo data after its retries, so nothing was recoverable from the datasets). Because page 10 failed while the deeper page 30 worked, the failures look like blocking/capacity noise, not a depth limit. Depth beyond 30 is unproven; 60 is unknown.
- **SLUTPRIS_ORDER: newest-first.** On page 30 all 35 `displayDate` values are in non-increasing order (2026-02-16 down to 2026-02-07, a nine-day span). A page 30 slice spanning only nine days at the far end also fits a date-descending order across pages (page 1 would then be the latest sales). Only one page parsed, so the across-page claim rests on that span, not on a second page.
- **NEGATIVE_LABEL_SHAPES: plus,null,en-dash.** This probe saw `plus` (28 cards) and `null` (7 cards) only: the 35 sold cards on page 30 had no below-asking sale. `en-dash` (U+2013, with a TrendDown icon) comes from the earlier 2026-10-12 session in 14.1-RESEARCH.md, not from this run. Hyphen and U+2212 were not seen anywhere. 14.1-01 already handles all three negative signs.
- **PRICE_REMARK_ICON_KEY: priceRemark.icon.key.** Value `TrendUp` on the plus cards; `TrendDown` was not seen in this run (see above).
- **CARD_150148_BALCONY: true.** The Booli search card for `/bostad/150148` lists `amenities` with keys `elevator` and `balcony`, so the card carries it; the for-sale side needs no detail fetch for the balcony.
- **APIFY_SPEND_USD: 0.3821 (runs-api).** Real bill from the Apify runs API for the 7 runs, about 4.20 SEK. The ledger is account-wide, so a concurrent run from another tool in the same window would also be counted; nothing else was running.

## Branch rules

  - 14.1-08 Task 1 runs the sold-detail sample enrichment when SOLD_DETAIL_BUILD_YEAR or SOLD_DETAIL_BALCONY is `present`; otherwise it skips enrichment, the model drops build year, uses the balcony prior (+1.9 %), and the SUMMARY says so (R-05, D-18).
  - 14.1-04 sets SLUTPRIS_MAX_PAGES_PER_SLICE to min(40, SLUTPRIS_PAGE_DEPTH) when depth is a number, and to 9 when it is `1-9` or `unknown`; size-band slicing is always on.
  - 14.1-04 stops paging a slice at the first page whose newest sold date is older than the cutoff only when SLUTPRIS_ORDER is `newest-first`; otherwise it walks to the page cap and filters by date.
  - `unknown` always takes the conservative branch above; no executor waits for a re-probe.

Consequences with these values: 14.1-08 runs the sold-detail sample enrichment (build year and balcony are both `present`); 14.1-04 uses SLUTPRIS_MAX_PAGES_PER_SLICE = min(40, 30) = 30 and stops a slice at the first page whose newest sold date is older than the cutoff.
