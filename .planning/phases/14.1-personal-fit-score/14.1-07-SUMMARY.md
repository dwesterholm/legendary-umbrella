# Phase 14.1 Plan 07: Profit composer, ranking rules and D-15 fixtures Summary

**fitScore now adds the vision-driven reno cost and a 5 %-capped taste bonus and flags sizes outside the fitted range; pre-rank, a 20-row vision window, the final order, a model-health check and a dominance check are tested pure functions; Daniel's six ordering rules run as fixtures.**

## What this plan does, in plain terms

The scorer already predicted a market price per flat. This plan finishes the arithmetic and the sorting.

1. **Profit.** Profit is the renovated resale value minus the expected final price (capped at 20 % of resale), minus the renovation cost, plus a taste bonus. A flat the pictures were read for uses what the pictures said (fresh 10 000 kr, all dated 427 500 kr). A flat nobody looked at uses the 105 000 kr middle bundle and is marked "ej bildtolkad". Taste (morning sun, near streets Daniel likes) adds at most 5 % of resale and never subtracts.
2. **Honesty flags.** A flat above 4 000 000 kr expected final is flagged and kept. A negative profit is shown as negative. A flat smaller than 35 m2 or larger than 80 m2 gets "storlek utanför modellen", because the model has no sales to learn from there; the profit is still shown, so a guess never looks like a measurement.
3. **Order.** Before any money is spent on picture reading, every flat is ranked for free (profit with the unknown reno cost, then the existing renovation-upside score, then Booli id). The top 20 get their pictures read. After that the real profit re-sorts the list. Two flats with the same profit always come out in the same order.
4. **Two checks.** Model health compares what flats cost on average to what the model says they are worth; outside 0.85 to 1.15 it should be reported, not corrected. The dominance check lists any flat ranked above a target that is no better on location, size, taste or reno cost and is not cheaper per m2, so D-14's "never below a clearly worse flat" becomes a number.
