## 7. Model recap (from 14.1-08)

- Fitted on 7 380 cleaned Stockholm sales from 2025-04 to 2026-10, 35 to 120 m2, four districts. It explains 61 % of the variation in price per m2 (R2 0.61) and is off by about 12 % on flats it has not seen (cross-validated error 0.116 in log terms).
- One floor up adds about 1.6 %. A ground floor or lower costs about 6.4 %.
- Each km farther from the nearest central hub costs about 0.7 %.
- Prices rise about 0.9 % a month (about 11 % a year).
- Neighbouring sales within 1 km get weight 1.09 (1.00 would mean the price follows the neighbours fully).
- Size effect is set to zero by the sign rule (D-18), although the data says flats over 60 m2 sell 3 to 5 % more per m2. Between 35 and 50 m2 this makes about no difference. Daniel decides whether to drop that rule.
- Balcony +1.9 % comes from the earlier study, not measured. Build year has no adjustment. Only 35 sold-detail pages were bought, and three of four age groups had under 20 sales.
- Bid premium, sold over asking, at the model price: Sodermalm +11.0 % (study +7 to +10 %), Kungsholmen +5.7 % (study +3 to +4 %), Vasastan +6.1 %, Ostermalm +4.0 %. The model uses a sloping form: the lower an asking price sits against the model price, the higher the expected bid.
