# Phase 14.3 Plan 04: Sun hours with neighbours in the way Summary

**Sun hours per facade for 21 Mar/Jun/Sep/Dec as a theoretical figure plus a low/nominal/high range from a ray-check against walls within 250 m, with building heights from OSM tags (levels x 3 + 2) or a flagged local median, DST-safe morning+midday windows, and all 76 snapshot flats (152 sides) in about 0,2 s.**

## Accomplishments

- `heightOf` / `buildingHeight`: a `height` tag as is; else storeys x 3 + 2 m (no roof allowance); else 3 m for sheds and similar; else the median of ordinary tagged neighbours whose centre is within 150,0 m (even count: mean of the middle two); else 17 m. Everything that is not a tag is `estimated` ("höjd uppskattad"). On the real cache 4 320 of 15 415 buildings (28 %) end up estimated, which matches the 73,8 % tag coverage the research measured.
- `wallsNear`: every edge of outer rings and courtyard holes of buildings with a vertex within radius + 50 m, in metres around the point, found through a 150 m grid (all 15 415 heights resolve in 49 ms).
- `sideHours`: 240 samples per date at 6-minute steps, counted as whole steps. Each lit sample is tested against all crossings once and evaluated for low (+3 m), nominal, high (-3 m, floored at 0) and the one-storey-lower flat (window only).
- Windows use `Intl.DateTimeFormat('sv-SE', { timeZone: 'Europe/Stockholm', hourCycle: 'h23' })`; the code never calls `getHours`. A by-hand count with UTC offsets 1, 2, 2, 1 hours matches exactly.

## Folkungagatan 61 (real 250 m cache, street side)

Bearing 166,2 degrees (south), flat at floor 5 so z = 16,5 m, 2 308 walls within reach, no estimated wall blocked a sample (`heightsEstimated` false). Hours:

|---|---|---|---|---|
| 21 Mar | 9,0 / 10,8 | 8,3 / 8,8 / 9,0 | 10,0 / 10,5 / 10,8 | 8,3 |
| 21 Jun | 8,0 / 9,7 | 8,0 / 8,0 / 8,0 | 9,7 / 9,7 / 9,7 | 8,0 |

| 21 Dec | 5,8 / 5,8 | 0,0 / 4,6 / 5,5 | 0,0 / 4,6 / 5,5 | 0,0 |

The research's reference figures (10-minute steps, roof allowance, full innerstan): theoretical Mar 10,7, Jun 9,7, Sep 10,8, Dec 5,7, and December 0 / 4,3 / 5,5. Ours differ by 0,1 h in theory (6-minute steps) and by 0,3 h at the December nominal (20 m neighbours here against 21,5 m there, because the roof allowance is left out on purpose). The December warning holds: 3 m of neighbour height moves this flat from no sun to 5,5 h, and one storey lower in the flat itself also gives 0,0 h.

The courtyard side of the same flat (north, 346,2 degrees) has 0,9 h of March, 7,7 h of June (morning and evening) and no December sun in theory; obstructed it is 0 in every scenario for March, September and December and 0 / 0 / 5,5 h of the day in June.

## Spread of the range across the snapshot (street side, real cache)

Of the flats with at least 1 h of theoretical window sun that date, the share whose window range (high minus low) is wider than 2 h: March 3 % (2 of 61), June 8 % (5 of 63), September 3 % (2 of 62), December 11 % (6 of 53). The research's wider +-3 m / +-6 m envelope gave 7 / 23 / 4 / 13 %; the plan's uniform +-3 m is the narrower claim, so these shares are the optimistic reading. 54 of the 74 flats have at least one estimated-height wall that blocks a sample.

## Decisions Made

- One bounding-box grid serves both the neighbour median (3 x 3 cells) and `wallsNear`; a centroid-only grid would miss long buildings.
- `heightsEstimated` is true only when an estimated wall would block on its own in the nominal case. A nearby estimate that never matters does not raise the flag.
- The geometry context lives in a `WeakMap` keyed by the index, so `OsmIndex` is unchanged and nothing leaks.
- `smallTypes` is typed `readonly string[]` so `includes` accepts any tag value.
- Plan 07 can pass any of the street or courtyard points to `wallsNear` and then `sideHours`; the engine does not know about `SunFact`.

- **Found during:** Task 2 GREEN (the engine passed 47 of 49)
- **Issue:** I expected a 30 m wall 20 m south of a z = 10 m flat to shade some of the June window, and I expected a 12 m wall's +-3 m envelope to move March. Measured with SunCalc: the ray clears 30 m at 20 m distance once the sun is above 45 degrees, and the June sun is above that from 08:12 to 13:24 UTC; farther along the wall the required angle is lower still (38,2 m away needs 27,6 degrees).
