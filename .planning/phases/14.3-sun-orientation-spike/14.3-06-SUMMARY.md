## Accomplishments

- `inferredSunScore` in `taste.ts`: per side, facade weight (east 1,0, south 0,7, west and north 0) x that side's obstruction factor; the best side counts (not the sum); times the source factor (0,5 for "fastighet/gata", 1 for "angiven" and "planritning") and the confidence; plus the 0,3 phrase credit once; capped at 1. The sun part is `sunMaxPct` (0,03) x that score, so it can never pass 3 % of resale. A grid test over every source, facade, confidence, factor and phrase combination confirms it.
- `tasteBonus` takes an optional `inferredSun`. Absent, null or source okänd gives today's `sunScore` path unchanged. The hub part is untouched by any sun input.
- `fit-score.ts`: `inferredSunForTaste(row.sun)` turns the stored fact into plain numbers; the obstruction factor per side is obstructed nominal / theoretical window hours on 21 March (`TASTE_SUN_OBSTRUCTION_DATE`), 1 when the side has no hours, no obstructed range or no theoretical sun, clamped to [0, 1]. The one `tasteBonus` call gained one line. Nothing else in the money path changed.
- Replay of the committed fixture snapshot (rows carry no sun data) prints byte-identical output before and after (`diff` empty, stderr identical), and `Folkungagatan 61` still prints.
- `npm run test`: 2 381 passed / 3 skipped (baseline 2 350 / 3, so 31 new tests). `npx tsc --noEmit` and `npm run lint` clean.

## Folkungagatan 61: what it is worth now

Worked by hand in the plan: street side south 0,7, shade factor 0,95, footprint only (x 0,5), confidence 1,00 gives 0,7 x 0,95 x 0,5 = 0,3325 of the 3 %, so sunPct 0,009975. The unit test pins exactly that.

On the real 250 m cache (the 12,9 MB innerstan file on this Mac) with the real hours from plan 04, the March window at the street side (bearing 166,2, floor 5) is 9,0 h theoretical and 8,8 h nominal obstructed, so the factor is 0,978 (the plan's 0,95 was an illustration). That gives:

| Folkungagatan 61 (Booli 150148), footprint-only data | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| Sun part of taste | 0,0000 of resale | 0,010267 of resale |
| Taste bonus | 98 265 kr (hub part only, 2,0 % of 4 913 242 kr resale) | 148 707 kr |
| Change | | **+50 443 kr** (about 1,0 % of resale) |
| Profit in the replay table | 653 506 kr | about 703 949 kr |

Still far under the 5 % total cap (245 662 kr for this flat). Across the 46 comparable rows of the fixture, using each row's real footprint and hours: 41 get a sun part (none had one before, because the fixture carries no stated sun text), the mean taste bonus moves from 37 957 kr to 66 304 kr, the largest single move is +94 045 kr and the smallest 0 kr. Rows with a stated facade would be unchanged against today (stated keeps 1,0), and the fixture happens to have none.

**The 0,5 street-side factor is an assumption, and Daniel can change it.** It exists because a footprint tells us which way the street side and the yard side of the building face, but not which side the living room and balcony are on. Giving half the best side's weight is a guess between "the rooms face the street" (full weight) and "they face the yard" (the yard side, often north, earns nothing). It is one line, `streetOnlyFactor: 0.5` in `TASTE_WEIGHTS` in `src/lib/discovery/taste.ts`. Setting it to 1,0 would double the footprint-only sun part (Folkungagatan 61 would gain about +100 886 kr instead of +50 443 kr); setting it to 0 turns the footprint off for taste. Plan 08 reports how much money the number moves across the whole table.

## Decisions Made

- The phrase credit is added after the scaling and the sum is capped at 1, matching the plan's formula. A stated "sol hela dagen" therefore keeps its worth even on a footprint-only side with low confidence, as today's `sunScore` treats it.
- Values above 1 for confidence or obstruction factor are clamped to 1 rather than trusted. The stored fact cannot hold them (the schema caps confidence at 1 and `inferredSunForTaste` clamps the factor), so this only guards direct callers.
- `taste.ts` keeps a local literal union for the source instead of importing the type from `sun-fact.ts`, so the "no runtime import from the sun modules" check is true by construction and a test pins it.
