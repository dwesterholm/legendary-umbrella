# Phase 14.3 Plan 03: Floor-plan compass probe Summary

**A separate Haiku call with a five-field strict-output schema read 12 real floor plans for 0,37 kr; it claimed a north arrow on 6 of 11 plans, but all 6 are the arrow at the front door, so the real compass count is 0 and the route stays closed.**

## What this means for Daniel (plain words)

The question was whether the floor plans that brokers put in the listing carry a north arrow, because then we could tell which way the balcony faces without guessing. The earlier research looked at 69 plans by eye and found none. This plan asked a cheap AI model to look at 12 plans as a second opinion.

The model said "yes, north arrow" on 6 of 11 floor plans. That would have passed the 10 % bar easily (54,5 %) and, taken at face value, would have opened the route and led to a 30 kr run over all candidates. I opened the six pictures. All six show a plain arrow at the front door that marks where you walk in. That is exactly the trap the prompt warned the model about, and it fell for it anyway. So there are no real compasses in this sample, which matches the earlier 0 of 69.

Consequence for the precedence order (stated text, then floor plan, then footprint/street): the floor-plan step stays empty. The footprint route from plans 01 and 02 is the only orientation source besides the listing text, and the balcony side stays "okänd". Had the route opened on the model's say-so, six flats would have gotten a wrong facade ranked above a right one. As a side effect worth knowing: the model gave Folkungagatan 61 (Booli 150148) "north up, balcony at the bottom" which works out to south, the correct answer, but only by luck. The picture has an entrance arrow at the top, so the model's "north" was the door arrow and the match with the Hemnet text is a coincidence, not evidence.
