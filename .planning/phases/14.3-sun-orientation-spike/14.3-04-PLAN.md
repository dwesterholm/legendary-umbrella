<objective>
Count how many hours of sun reach a facade, first with no buildings around (theoretical) and then with the neighbours' walls in the way (obstructed), for 21 March, June, September and December, as a low / nominal / high range.

Purpose: SUN-03, the spike proper. OpenStreetMap has a storey count for about 74 % of innerstan buildings; the rest get a local estimate. Because building heights are uncertain by about one storey, a single number would claim more than we know (Folkungagatan 61 in December ranges from 0 to 5.5 hours depending on a 3 m change across the street), so every obstructed figure is a range. The engine is pure and works on any wall list; plan 07 feeds it from the cache.
Output: heights and a wall lookup in osm-buildings.ts; sun-hours.ts; tests including DST, boundaries and speed.
</objective>
