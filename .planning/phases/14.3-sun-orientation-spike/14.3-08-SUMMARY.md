# Phase 14.3 Plan 08: Sun evidence runs and the D-17 sun clause Summary

**A real run of the weekly command now gives 74 of 76 flats (and 75 of 77 in the planning snapshot) a facade, 97,4 % against the 80 % bar, so 14.2's open sun clause is met; Folkungagatan 61 comes out as street side south with 8,8 h of March sun after the neighbours, and its taste bonus rises 51 401 kr.**

## What it means for Daniel

Before this phase the table knew which way a flat faces for only 2 flats in 77, because brokers rarely write it. Now it knows for 74 of 76 in today's list, from where the building stands on the map and which side the street is on. For 72 of those 74 the answer comes from the map, for 2 from the broker's own text. The two flats without an answer are Reimersholmsgatan 39 and Heleneborgsgatan 30A, where the map has no street with the same name within 35 m of the listing coordinates.

How far to trust it: the map says which side is the street side and which is the yard side. It cannot say which side the living room and balcony are on. That is why the taste bonus counts only half the weight for a map-only facade (the 0,5 factor, see the assumptions below). In the only two cases where a broker did state a direction, both said west, and in both the west side is the yard side, not the street side. That is two flats, too few to prove the street guess wrong, and the direction it points is the one the half weight already hedges against.

The hours are stated as a range. For 21 March, 68 of the 70 flats that get any real sun have a range no wider than 2 hours, so "plus or minus 1 hour" holds for 97 % of them. For 21 June it holds for 82 %, for 21 December for 89 %. Where it does not hold, the table prints "usäker" next to the hours.

Nothing was tuned: no constant, weight or threshold changed because of these numbers.

## Assumptions Daniel may overrule

Each is a guess the numbers lean on. Where the logs allow, the consequence is in kronor or hours.

1. **Street-side factor 0,5 (A4).** A map-only facade counts half in the taste bonus, because the map shows the street and yard sides but not where the living room is. One line: `streetOnlyFactor: 0.5` in `TASTE_WEIGHTS`, `src/lib/discovery/taste.ts`. At 1,0 the average flat gets another 24 931 kr of taste and Folkungagatan 61 another 51 401 kr; at 0 the map sun is off and the table returns to 14.2's taste column. The two stated-west flats put the stated side on the yard side both times, a small sample that supports keeping the half weight rather than raising it.
2. **Booli floor numbering: floor 1 is the first floor above ground.** If Booli's number is off by one floor, the window hours move by more than 1 hour for 0 of 70 flats in March, 2 of 74 in June, 0 of 71 in September and 5 of 53 in December (replay). Flats without a floor (Kungsbro Strand 19) get no neighbour-shade check at all.
3. **No roof allowance.** A top-floor window under a roof overhang or a dormer is treated as open to the sky above it. The logs do not measure how many flats this touches, so I cannot give a number.
4. **250 m radius for neighbouring buildings.** Anything further away is ignored. Not measured in this run.
5. **Heights are partly estimated.** 26 % of buildings in innerstan have no height in the map; in the logs, 54 of the 74 shaded flats in the replay (53 of 73 live) have at least one such neighbour, and the row says "höjd uppskattad". Official 3D height data was not obtainable inside the spike. Follow-up (not started): ask Stockholms stad about a licence for their 3D model, and look once at geotorget.lantmateriet.se.
