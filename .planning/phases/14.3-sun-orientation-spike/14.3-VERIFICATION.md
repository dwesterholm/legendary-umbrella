### Judging the compass override

Rule as written (SUN-02, D-03/R-01/R-09): go only if >= 10 % of plans show a real compass. The script's mechanical verdict was "go" (6 of 11 = 54.5 %); the executor overrode it to "dropped". My judgment: justified, not a way of dodging a failed measurement.

- The measurement's purpose is "do broker plans carry a north arrow". Research (14.3-RESEARCH.md line 172) viewed 69 plans by eye: 0 north arrows, many entrance arrows, and named this exact lookalike as Pitfall 9 before the probe ran.
- The probe log itself looks like noise: "norrpil ja" answers come with norr 180, 270, 90 and 0 on plans that mostly show an entrance arrow, and the model reports 0 degrees with 0.95 confidence on every plan it says has no arrow.
- I downloaded the Booli 150148 plan (flagged "norrpil ja, norr 0") and viewed it: a single bold black arrow at the top wall pointing into the hall door, no letter N, no rose. That is an entrance arrow.
- Consequence of being wrong would have been the opposite of the executor's choice: wiring entrance arrows in as north would feed false bearings into precedence.
- The decision is recorded openly (JSON `audit` block, Sol report line "modellen läste 6 av 11, 54,5 %, men alla 6 är ytterdörrspilar", `väg stängd`) so the spreadsheet reader cannot mistake it for an unmeasured route.

Residual: 5 of the 6 flagged plans are verified only by the executor's and research phase's eyes. Listed as an optional human spot-check above. No override entry is required because the recorded verdict (`dropped`) satisfies the roadmap wording "closed after the probe, with an audit".
