<objective>
Settle with a model read, for at most 2 kr, whether broker floor plans carry a compass. The research looked at all 69 plans by eye and found none; this plan checks that verdict with the model, records it, and closes the route unless at least 10 % of plans show one.

Purpose: D-03 / R-01 and the plan part of SUN-02. If a compass were common it would outrank the footprint in the precedence order, so the number matters, and an entrance arrow read as north would put a wrong facade above a correct one. The probe runs as its own small call with its own cache so the 77 picture reads already paid for (about 33 kr) stay valid.
Output: sun-floorplan.ts with tests, the probe script, a synthetic test plan, the live smoke, the 12-plan probe, and the verdict written into code and evidence.
</objective>
