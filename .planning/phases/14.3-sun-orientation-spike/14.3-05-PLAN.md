<objective>
Decide, for every flat, which source of its facing direction wins and how sure we are, and say so when two sources disagree.

Purpose: SUN-02 (D-05). Three sources can say which way a flat faces: the broker's text, a compass on the floor plan, and the building footprint. They are not equally good, so a fixed order decides, and a disagreement of more than 90 degrees halves the confidence and shows both instead of hiding one. The footprint gives a street side and a courtyard side but not which one the living room faces; a stated facade picks the side when one is near it.
Output: resolveSunFact, statedFacadesOf and SUN_PRECEDENCE_PARAMS in sun-fact.ts, with tests.
</objective>
