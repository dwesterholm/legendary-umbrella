## Decisions Made

- `normalizeStreetName` is defined in `osm-buildings.ts` and re-exported from `sun-facade.ts`; the plan listed it as a sun-facade export, and this keeps that true without a circular import.
- Boundary tests compare at the stored 1-decimal precision (so 25,0 m snaps, 35,0 m qualifies, 10,0 m counts as near, whatever the float noise).
- Group ties (equal weight) fall to the nearer street at stored precision, then the lower bearing. In the corner test the east front wins over the south front.
- `sunFactSchema` has a refinement: `unknownReason` is set exactly when `source` is "okänd".
- Relation outers after the first get ids `relation/<id>#2`, `#3` (1-based position).

## Deviations from Plan

### Auto-fixed Issues

**1. [Rule 3 - Blocking] Fixture size**
- **Found during:** Task 1
- **Issue:** The plan asks for 2-space JSON under 300 KB. A plain 2-space dump of the research cut is 403 KB, and 333 KB with each point on one line.
- **Fix:** Dropped the optional `bounds` field from every element (the contract marks it optional and nothing reads it) and kept each geometry point on one line. Result 294 683 bytes, still 2-space indented structure, still only OSM data plus the contract fields.
- **Files modified:** `src/lib/discovery/__fixtures__/osm-folkungagatan-61.fixture.json`
- **Committed in:** `2b004b8`

**2. [Rule 1 - Bug] Float noise decided a tie and a confidence boundary**
- **Found during:** Task 2 GREEN (two RED tests still failed after the first implementation)
- **Issue:** A symmetric corner block picked "south" over "east" because the two street distances differed by 1e-15; and a road exactly 10,0 m away counted as "far" (10.000000000000002).
- **Fix:** Distance comparisons for the tie-break and the confidence factors use the stored 1-decimal value.
- **Files modified:** `src/lib/discovery/sun-facade.ts`
- **Committed in:** `27e680e`
