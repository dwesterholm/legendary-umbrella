### CR-01: One bad OSM `height` tag (Ringen centrum, 151,7 m) corrupts the shade figures of nearby flats

**Files modified:** `src/lib/discovery/osm-buildings.ts`, `src/lib/discovery/osm-buildings.test.ts`, `scripts/fit-score.ts`
**Commit:** ba5e81f
**Status:** fixed: requires human verification (logic rule; the landmark list in the second bullet below needs a human eye)
**Applied fix:** A height tag is "orimlig" when it is more than 2,5 times the largest of the storey formula (levels x 3 + 2), the local median and the innerstan typical height (17 m), or over 60 m on at most 8 storeys. An orimlig tag is replaced by the storey formula (else the local median), marked `estimated` (prints "höjd uppskattad") and `suspect`, and is kept out of neighbours' medians. The Sol source line now prints `höjd orimlig <n> (ersatt med uppskattning)`, and `suspectHeights(index)` lists the ids.

Two deliberate differences from the suggested rule:
- The 17 m floor in the reference. With only `max(levels formula, local median)` the rule flagged real landmarks such as Vasamuseet (41 m), Stockholms slott (39 m), Rosenbad and Katarinahissen, because the local median in a low-rise pocket can be as low as 5,75 m (Eriksdalsbadet). The floor removed 10 of 21 such false flags.
- Exemption by tag type (church, cathedral, chapel, mosque, synagogue, temple, tower, chimney; `man_made` tower, chimney, mast, lighthouse, water_tower, silo, communications_tower) in addition to an id allowlist (`trustedTallIds`: Fritt fall way/1122922291, Katapulten way/1122927971, industrial "torn" way/877287650). Listing about 15 churches by id would break at the next download.

Replay result (obstructed window hours, nominal, only the Ringen change): Dalslandsgatan 3 June 0,7 h to 8,0 h; Tjurbergsgatan 38 (two listings) March 0,8 h / 0,4 h to 2,9 h / 2,5 h; Blekingegatan 36 courtyard March 2,6 h to 5,4 h; Tjurbergsgatan 34 March 0,0 h to 1,0 h. Hornsbergs Strand 15F and Möregatan 6 only changed their "höjd uppskattad" flag.

**Still flagged, not verified (10 buildings).** The rule rejects 11 tags on the real cache. One is Ringen. The other ten look like tall buildings whose `building:levels` tag is the doubtful part, and I could not confirm any of them without looking at them: Göta Arkhuset way/37280553 (60 m, 4 levels), way/39869639 office (85,5 m, 7 levels), way/193833946 apartments (68 m, 7 levels), Nordiska museet way/348345482 (64 m, 3 levels), Sankt Erikspalatset way/1360293334 (58 m), relation/1658671 office (53 m, 6 levels), and the Kajen group relation/14462050, relation/16173229, relation/16173230, relation/16173231 (75 to 87,5 m, no levels). Effect on the 78 flats of the 2026-10-13 snapshot, measured by trusting each one in turn: none moves a nominal hours figure. Fatburstrappan 18 (Göta Arkhuset) changes a low/high range, Möregatan 6 and Hornsbergs Strand 15F change only the estimated flag. If a human confirms any of them as real, add its id to `HEIGHT_PARAMS.trustedTallIds`.

### WR-01: The facade (window) point can land inside a building

**Files modified:** `src/lib/discovery/sun-facade.ts`, `src/lib/discovery/sun-facade.test.ts`, `src/lib/discovery/sun-hours.test.ts`
**Commit:** 9acce0a
**Status:** fixed: requires human verification (geometry heuristic; courtyard figures moved noticeably)
**Applied fix:** Measured first: 10 of 152 derived points sat inside a footprint on the snapshot, and 14 more lay exactly on a neighbouring wall of the same building (the pushed point of a wall that ends at an inside corner runs along the next wall). The point is now pushed 1 m (`outwardPushM` 1, was 0,3), taken at least 1 m from either end of its wall (`cornerMarginM`), and accepted only when it is in no building and at least 0,5 m from every wall (`clearanceM`). A wall that fails hands over to the next-nearest one. If none passes, the point is `null` and the side keeps theoretical hours only (no false zero). `streetPoint` is therefore `{lat,lng} | null`; one flat (Heleneborgsgatan 36B, whose street walls are party walls) now shows "-" in the obstructed cell instead of a figure computed from inside the neighbour. After the fix 0 of 151 points are inside a building and the closest any point is to a wall is 0,9 m.

**Deviation from the suggested fix.** The guidance said to skip the own building's walls in the ray test. I did not, because the wings across a courtyard belong to the own building and are the real shade of a courtyard flat: skipping them would show a ground-floor courtyard flat as sunny. With the point guaranteed outside every building, the ray can no longer start inside a wall, which was the actual defect. Covered by tests: party wall behind the courtyard side (point is null, hours fall back to theoretical), next-nearest wall fallback, inside-corner spot, and a property test over the real Folkungagatan cut (more than 100 points, all outside and 0,9 m clear).

Replay effect: the street side moves by 0,1 h or less for most flats (point moved 0,7 m). The courtyard side moves more and mostly downwards, because the old points sat on the wall itself, for example Kristinebergsvägen 3 courtyard March 7,7 h to 2,8 h, Hallandsgatan 30 courtyard June 8,0 h to 3,7 h, Idungatan 5 courtyard June 9,0 h to 3,8 h, Klippgatan 14E courtyard June 1,8 h to 0,0 h. Tobaksspinnargatan 10 courtyard went from a false 0,0 h to 3,6 h in March. A human should glance at a few courtyard figures against the map.

### WR-02: Inconsistent metres-per-degree constants

**Files modified:** `src/lib/discovery/osm-buildings.ts`, `src/lib/discovery/osm-buildings.test.ts`, `src/lib/discovery/sun-facade.ts`
**Commit:** ded9aa7
**Applied fix:** New exported `metresPerDegree(lat)` (WGS84 series: about 111 402 m per degree of latitude and 56 944 m per degree of longitude at 59,33 N) used by `projectFrom`, `unprojectFrom` and the snap prefilter in `sun-facade.ts`; the duplicated constants are gone. Tests compare against ellipsoid radii of curvature: 300 m due north and due east agree to 0,1 m (old code: 297,8 m north), a 300 m diagonal measures 424,26 m within 0,15 m with a bearing of 45 degrees within 0,05. The "few centimetres over 300 m" comment now says "within about 0,1 m". Replay effect (measured on top of WR-01): 34 of 78 flats show a changed figure, almost all by 0,1 to 0,2 h. The largest are Hornsbergs Strand 15F in June (3,3 h to 4,3 h) and Fatburstrappan 18 (0,0 h to 0,8 h in March), where a ray that used to graze a wall edge now clears it. No flat changed source or lost its facade.

**Commit:** b57e7a8
**Applied fix:** New `computeSunFactSafe` wraps `computeSunFact` (and the floor-plan cache read, passed as a function). An exception becomes the okänd fact with reason `fel` (`SUN_FAILURE_REASON`) and returns the error NAME only; `makeSunStage` prints one `OBS: sol kunde inte beräknas för <adress> (<ErrorName>); okänd (fel), körningen fortsätter` line per failing flat and carries on. Tests: a broken index makes `computeSunFact` throw and the safe version return okänd/fel; a throwing plan function is caught and its message never appears in the result; the next flat is still computed.

### WR-04: The 2 kr probe cap is per invocation and defeated by a non-finite cost

**Files modified:** `src/lib/discovery/sun-floorplan.ts`, `src/lib/discovery/sun-floorplan.test.ts`, `scripts/sun-floorplan-probe.ts`
**Commit:** 7b186b0
**Status:** fixed: requires human verification (the script-side wiring cannot be tested without a paid call)
**Applied fix:** (1) The cache file records `spentSekTotal` (optional, so older files still load); the cap is checked against `max(recorded total, sum of stored reads) + this run + estimate`, and `cacheNext` carries the new total, also across prompt-version drops. The real cache (0,37 kr spent) therefore counts. (2) A call that returns a NaN, infinite, zero or negative cost is counted at the 0,05 kr estimate; a non-finite cap allows no call. (3) `asBilledFailure` turns a `messages.parse` failure that happened after the answer arrived (ZodError, SyntaxError) into a `FloorPlanCallError` billing the estimate; HTTP and connection errors (`Anthropic.APIError`) still count as unbilled. (4) `--smoke` checks the cap before its synthetic call, records its cost, and saves the cache even when the real read failed. The probe prints this run's spend and the running total. Sixteen of the new tests fail on the old code, including the NaN case (old code made a third call under a 0,10 kr cap).

Not exercised: the smoke and probe paths with `RUN_LLM_EVALS=1`, because they spend money. Only the skip path (`--smoke` without the gate, exit 0) was run.

### WR-05: A cache with valid metadata but unusable geometry stays "fresh" for 31 days

**Files modified:** `src/lib/discovery/osm-cache.ts`, `src/lib/discovery/osm-cache.test.ts`, `scripts/fit-score.ts`
**Commit:** 1dd1acc
**Applied fix:** `loadOsmCacheRaw` (and so `readOsmCacheMeta`, which feeds the refresh decision) now requires geometry that `parseOsmExtract` accepts and at least one building; otherwise the result is `{ error: "oläsbar" }`, i.e. treated as missing, and the next run downloads again. The okänd reason comes from the load failure through `osmCacheUnknownReason` (three fixed labels: "OSM-cache saknas", "OSM-cache ogiltig", "OSM-cache oläsbar"), and the Sol line adds `kör: npx tsx scripts/osm-download.ts --force` when the cache cannot be used. Tests: valid metadata with a latitude of 99 reads as oläsbar and is downloaded again although brand new (old code: "fresh", no download); an empty-building file is unusable; a failed download leaves the bad file byte-identical and reports `failed-no-cache`; a good file of the same age is still fresh. The cost is one extra `parseOsmExtract` (about 76 ms on the real file) per cache read.

Not exercised end to end: the live-run wiring of the reason into `makeSunStage` (a live run costs money); it is covered by `tsc` and by the unit tests of the two helper functions.
